# Seven Minutes After Midnight

Genre: Boys' Love / Adult Romance / Time-Slip Mystery / Historical Thriller / Korean Manhwa

## Premise

Daniel Soriano, a present-day museum conservator, discovers that the abandoned railway cafe inside San Aurelio Junction returns to life for exactly seven minutes after midnight.

Inside Cafe Siete he meets Tomas Rivera, a baker living in 1986. Daniel experiences the station in 2026 while Tomas experiences the same physical place forty years earlier. During the connection they can see one another, share the counter, briefly touch, and exchange small objects through the cafe's narrow service window, but neither man can fully cross into the other's time.

Their seven-minute meetings become an adult romance built from letters, pastries, careful jokes, impossible contact, and the growing fear that the archive has already recorded Tomas's death in a deliberately set station fire.

Daniel must eventually choose how hard to fight history when changing the past may destroy the abandoned cafe, erase the circumstances that allowed them to meet, or alter his own memories of Tomas.

## Tone Lock

This is a mature BL time-slip romance with grounded historical-mystery pressure: quiet adult longing, old railway timetables, flour on sleeves, handwritten notes, archival folders, damaged newsprint, rain on station glass, service-window contact, and dread created by a clock that only grants seven minutes.

Keep Daniel and Tomas emotionally adult and restrained. Their attraction develops through attention, proximity, work habits, exchanged objects, interrupted jokes, hands at the threshold, and the pain of running out of time.

Avoid school-romance energy, chibi comedy, palace fantasy, xianxia styling, gothic revenge, supernatural train mythology, ghost-conductor rules, corporate-system overlays, generic portal fantasy, and unrestricted time-travel wish fulfillment.

## Dual-Era Environment Rule — Absolute

San Aurelio Junction and Cafe Siete are the same physical architecture seen in different eras.

The 1986 and 2026 versions are not independent locations that may be redesigned separately.

Permanent structural relationships remain locked across eras:
- station footprint and main circulation
- cafe position beside the ticket hall
- cafe counter geometry
- service-window location and opening
- walls, doors, windows, major openings, and fixed architecture
- clock/menu-wall relationship
- physically valid camera axes and reverse angles

Era state may change through age, fire damage, abandonment, repair, removed furniture, redevelopment barriers, grime, lighting, signage, and ordinary historically plausible replacement. Structural drift is not allowed unless the story explicitly establishes that the building was physically modified.

## Midnight Cafe Rules — Canon

1. The abandoned railway cafe returns to life from 12:00 AM to 12:07 AM each night when the connection opens.
2. Daniel experiences the cafe from 2026. Tomas experiences it from 1986.
3. During the seven minutes both men can perceive the same threshold space, but neither can fully cross into the other's time.
4. The service window is the only reliable exchange point for small objects.
5. Living bodies cannot pass through the service window. Hands may make limited contact; instability increases near the threshold.
6. Objects passed from 1986 to 2026 arrive aged by roughly forty years unless the active interval temporarily protects them. Paper yellows, ink fades, pastry dries, photographs warp, and metal tarnishes.
7. Objects passed from 2026 to 1986 initially look unnervingly new and begin adapting to the older time after the connection closes.
8. Direct attempts to warn or force major historical changes weaken the connection.
9. Indirect clues, coded notes, and ordinary objects may exert less pressure, but the cafe responds to intent rather than wording alone.
10. The cafe clock records the remaining connection. Lost time is story-critical evidence and may not reset casually.
11. The fire at San Aurelio Junction is treated as a fixed event by surviving archives, but the archive may be incomplete, altered, or deliberately falsified.
12. Saving Tomas too strongly may remove the circumstances that leave the cafe abandoned in 2026, threatening Daniel's access to the connection and possibly his memories.

## Chapter 1 Canon Lock

Chapter 1 is `The Cafe That Opened For Seven Minutes`.

Mandatory story path:
- Daniel investigates suspicious San Aurelio archival records and enters the abandoned station at 11:41 PM.
- The station's stopped clock reads 12:07.
- Daniel enters Cafe Siete and finds the scratched name `TOMAS`.
- Daniel discovers the corroded brass Cafe Siete token marked `7`.
- The cafe clock snaps from 12:07 to 12:00 and the seven-minute connection opens.
- Daniel meets Tomas Rivera in the living 1986 cafe.
- their first accidental contact occurs at the tray/counter boundary
- they establish 1986 versus 2026
- the clock advances visibly through 12:01–12:07
- Tomas denies scratching his name on Daniel's ruined glass
- unstable counter contact causes a painful wrist catch and the service window opens
- Tomas sends fresh bread/saucer forward; it ages forty years
- Daniel sends an acid-free sleeve backward containing his identity, museum, year, and `I am real.`
- Tomas keeps it in his apron pocket and answers emotionally that he is real too
- Lilia is heard from the 1986 kitchen
- Tomas names Arturo Salcedo, a white-suited broker visiting station offices; the southbound signal also failed
- at 12:06 the connection begins visibly collapsing
- Daniel writes `DO NOT TELL ANYONE ABOUT ME. / KEEP THIS HIDDEN. / COME BACK TOMORROW.`
- their fingers touch through the service window without the earlier pain
- at 12:07 the cafe returns to abandoned 2026 state
- Daniel's warning page returns forty years older with Tomas's added line `I will bring better bread.`
- Daniel discovers the second scratched warning: `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.`
- the station clock remains fixed at 12:07

Do not compress away or reorder these causal facts during vertical adaptation.
