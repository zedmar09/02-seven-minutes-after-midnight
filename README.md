# Seven Minutes After Midnight

Genre: Boys' Love / Adult Romance / Time-Slip Mystery / Historical Thriller / Korean Manhwa

## Premise

Daniel Soriano, a present-day museum conservator, discovers that abandoned Cafe Siete inside San Aurelio Junction returns to life for a fragile midnight interval. Across the same service-window threshold he meets Tomas Rivera, a baker living in 1986. They can see/touch through constrained co-registered space, exchange small objects, and speak only while the connection remains open.

Daniel eventually learns Tomas died in the station fire recorded in the archive. Attempts to interfere with history can shorten future connection time and may erase the abandoned conditions that allow them to meet.

The story is an adult BL romance built around fragile midnight meetings, archival evidence, railway history, object aging across forty years, and a human conspiracy behind the San Aurelio fire.
