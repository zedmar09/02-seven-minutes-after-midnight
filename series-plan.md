# Seven Minutes After Midnight — Series Plan

This file stores the creator-facing long-running roadmap and mystery spine for `Seven Minutes After Midnight`.

The active visual-production format is the vertical `Manhwa/` pipeline. Do not create future arc/chapter folders until that arc/chapter enters active production.

## Long-Running Target

Preferred long-form shape:

- total arcs: 7
- total chapters: 105
- average arc length: 15 chapters
- format: mature BL time-slip romance / historical mystery / emotional thriller / grounded railway conspiracy

The story may expand toward 120–150 chapters only if new material deepens the time-slip system, witnesses, altered archives, or connected railway locations without displacing Daniel and Tomas as the emotional center.

## Core Long-Range Invariants

- Daniel and Tomas remain the central relationship.
- San Aurelio Junction remains the emotional and causal anchor even if later arcs reveal other crossings.
- The service-window connection is specific, fragile, and rule-bound; it never becomes unrestricted portal travel.
- The seven-minute clock and any lost-minute degradation remain consequential.
- Historical alteration has costs. Solving a mystery cannot become consequence-free timeline wish fulfillment.
- The official fire record can be incomplete/falsified, but contradictions must resolve through evidence, witness behavior, or time-slip causality rather than arbitrary retcon.
- Arturo Salcedo and the station-land conspiracy begin as human wrongdoing; supernatural mechanics must not erase human responsibility.
- Romance progression must remain adult, earned, and integrated with the mystery.

### Arc 1 Mystery Spine

- Chapter 1 proves the connection and ends with `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.`
- Arturo Salcedo is already present in the 1986 station orbit and tied to redevelopment interests.
- the southbound signal failure is an early mechanical clue, not decorative atmosphere
- service-corridor fire origin conflicts with the official electrical-fault explanation
- missing/cut archive names and altered witness material imply deliberate record manipulation
- Tomas's ordinary work objects and notes become evidence precisely because no one expected a cafe worker's records to matter
- Daniel's conservation expertise is plot machinery: paper age, glue, ink, cuts, scorch, moisture, copying, and provenance should expose lies
- romance and investigation advance together; every attempt to warn Tomas risks shrinking the connection

## Expansion Option

If the story needs more space after Chapter 105, strong expansion slots include:
- between Arcs 2–3: object-exchange mysteries and falsified archive cases
- between Arcs 3–4: witnesses connected to the station fire
- between Arcs 4–5: a small number of distinct railway crossings with separate emotional cases
- between Arcs 6–7: carefully placed 1986 chapters showing Tomas's decisions before the fire

Avoid expansion that turns the story into an anthology and avoid exceeding 150 chapters unless the larger railway-history conspiracy has enough recurring characters, factions, and causal structure to support it.
