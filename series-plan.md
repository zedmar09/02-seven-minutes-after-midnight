# Seven Minutes After Midnight - Series Plan

This file stores the long-running manga/manhwa/manhua roadmap for `Seven Minutes After Midnight`.

The current production structure should stay focused on the chapters being written or generated. Do not create future arc folders until we are ready to start that arc.

## Long-Running Target

Recommended long-running version:

- Total arcs: 7
- Total chapters: 105
- Average arc length: 15 chapters
- Format: mature BL time-slip romance, historical mystery, emotional thriller, and grounded railway conspiracy

This is the preferred 100+ chapter target. The story can expand beyond this only if the time-slip mystery grows into multiple stations, altered archives, and recurring historical factions without losing Daniel and Tomas as the emotional center.

| 5 | `05-Arc-A-Cafe-Without-Midnight` | 61-75 | 15 | Daniel changes too much; the cafe remains, but Tomas no longer remembers him clearly. |
| 6 | `06-Arc-The-Year-That-Burned-Twice` | 76-90 | 15 | 1986 begins repeating in fragments as Tomas investigates from the past and Daniel fights a collapsing archive in 2026. |
| 7 | `07-Arc-Seven-Minutes-Before-Morning` | 91-105 | 15 | Final conflict: Daniel and Tomas must choose between saving Tomas, preserving the cafe, or accepting a future neither timeline can fully keep. |

## Expansion Option

If the series needs more room after the 105-chapter plan, expand toward 120-150 chapters by adding focused case arcs between major timeline arcs.

Good expansion slots:

- Between Arc 2 and Arc 3: more object-exchange mysteries and false archive records.
- Between Arc 3 and Arc 4: more witnesses connected to the station fire.
- Between Arc 4 and Arc 5: one or two other railway cafes with separate emotional cases.
- Between Arc 6 and Arc 7: 1986 flashback chapters showing Tomas's choices before the fire.

Avoid stretching the story past 150 chapters unless it gains several strong recurring factions, other time-slip locations, and a larger railway-history conspiracy. The romance should still feel rare, fragile, and centered on the seven-minute meetings.

## Future Folder Rule

When a new arc starts, create it under `Comics/` using this format:

```text
Comics/
  02-Arc-The-Letters-That-Should-Not-Exist/
    README.md
    Chapter-16-Title-In-Camel-Case/
      chapter.md
      page-001-chatgpt-image-prompt.md
      1.webp
```

Each chapter folder should contain:

- `chapter.md`
- page prompt Markdown files
- cover prompt Markdown files when needed
- final reader images as WebP files
