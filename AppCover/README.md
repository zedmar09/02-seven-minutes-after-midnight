## App Metadata

Title: Seven Minutes After Midnight

Genre: Boys' love / adult men / time-slip romance / historical mystery / emotional thriller

Short synopsis:
Present-day museum conservator Daniel Soriano discovers an abandoned railway cafe that returns to life for exactly seven minutes every midnight. There he meets Tomas Rivera, a cheerful long-haired baker living forty years in the past. As their letters, pastries, and midnight touches become love, Daniel uncovers evidence that Tomas died in a deliberately set station fire, and saving him may erase the cafe that made them possible.
