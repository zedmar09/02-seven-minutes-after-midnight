# Character Card Standard

Every recurring character canonical card must establish one stable adult identity suitable for reuse across vertical Manhwa production.

## Required Views

Prefer a clean reference sheet containing:
- front or near-front portrait
- three-quarter portrait
- side/profile identity view
- full-body standing view
- back/three-quarter-back view when hair/wardrobe construction requires it
- restrained expression range
- identity-critical accessories/marks/details

## Required Locks

Define and preserve:
- exact adult age presentation
- face geometry
- eye/nose/jaw relationships
- permanent scars/marks and anatomical side
- hair length, part, silhouette, tie/cord, color
- build/height relationship
- wardrobe construction and local colors
- glasses, watches, jewelry, gloves, apron, hair cord, or other recurring accessories
- matte material family

## Anatomy

One coherent human body per view. Reject extra/missing/duplicated limbs, impossible joints, detached hands/feet, inconsistent shoulder/hip width, or close-up details incompatible with the full-body design.

## Style — Absolute

Strict flat 2D human-drawn Korean manhwa/webtoon illustration. Use clean intentional lines, flat colors, simple hard-edged cel shading only, matte materials, and neutral reference lighting.

Reject photorealism, semi-realism, 3D/CGI, glossy/plastic skin or hair, cinematic lighting, bloom, rim-light glamour, depth-of-field blur, painterly rendering, or over-polished AI beauty treatment.

## Reference Background

Use a simple neutral light background with no cinematic scene, no environmental storytelling, no dramatic weather, and no effects that obscure identity.

## Approval

A generated image becomes authority only after visual QA approval and conversion/storage as the exact repository `.webp` path referenced by production Markdown.
