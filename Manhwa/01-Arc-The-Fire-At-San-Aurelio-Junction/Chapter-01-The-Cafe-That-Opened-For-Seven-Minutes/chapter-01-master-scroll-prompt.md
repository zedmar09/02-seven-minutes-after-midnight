This file governs the full Chapter 1 adaptation. Individual production-strip prompts repeat their local required state and exact text; this master never authorizes skipping those local locks.

## Output Target

Create `The Cafe That Opened For Seven Minutes` as ONE continuous vertical full-color Korean manhwa chapter assembled from 18 sequentially approved technical strips.

Technical strips are invisible production chunks. No reader-visible pages, strip numbers, beat IDs, scene labels, QA labels, or production metadata.

## Absolute Visual Style

STRICT FLAT 2D HUMAN-DRAWN FULL-COLOR KOREAN MANHWA/WEBTOON.

Clean intentional linework, flat matte colors, restrained hard-edged cel shading, stable adult anatomy, natural hands, stable character identity, physically coherent architecture, mobile readability.

## Character Lock

### Daniel Soriano

Age 36, warm brown skin, medium-tall slim adult specialist build, long narrow face, tired dark eyes, close-cropped black curls with early silver at temples, stable nose-bridge scar, thin rectangular work glasses when established, slate-blue chore jacket, conservation workflow/hand behavior.

Performance path: precise/dry → skeptical → controlled wonder → vulnerable warmth → investigative urgency → post-loss controlled emotion → final dread.

### Tomas Rivera

Age 28, sun-warm tan skin, lean baker build, bright dark eyes, long dark wavy hair past shoulders tied low at nape/back with jade-green cord, face-framing strands, cream cafe shirt, brown waist apron, practical work body language.

Performance path: warm/teasing with hidden fear → curious → shaken by proof → vulnerable warmth → fear increasingly visible as clock collapses → private tenderness at final contact.

## Architecture Lock

1986 and 2026 are the same physical San Aurelio/Cafe Siete architecture.

Permanent structure does not independently redesign across eras. Cafe counter, narrow service-window opening, cafe entry/glass, clock/menu-wall, major walls/openings, and station/cafe relationship remain stable.

The service window is never large enough for a body.

## Exact Chapter Causality

1. Daniel's archival investigation proves official San Aurelio records have been manipulated.
2. At 11:41 PM he enters abandoned station; station clock is stopped at 12:07.
3. He finds Cafe Siete and old scratch `TOMAS`.
4. He retrieves one corroded brass Cafe Siete token marked `7`.
5. Cafe clock moves 12:07→12:00; connection opens; token drops.
6. Tomas appears in living 1986 cafe carrying tray; first accidental finger contact occurs while tray is stabilized.
7. Daniel/Tomas establish 1986 versus 2026 and introduce themselves; clock reaches 12:01.
8. Tomas sees/denies Daniel's future scratch; clock reaches 12:02.
9. Counter instability causes Tomas to catch Daniel's wrist; painful contact; service window opens.
10. Tomas sends one fresh roll on one white saucer forward; same objects age forty years; clock reaches 12:03.
11. Daniel sends one acid-free sleeve backward with identity/museum/year/`I am real.`; Tomas pockets same sleeve and says `Then I am real too.`; clock 12:04.
12. Daniel's flashlight falls; Lilia is heard offscreen from 1986 kitchen.
13. Clock 12:05; Daniel asks unusual events; Tomas names white-suited Arturo Salcedo and failed southbound signal.
14. Daniel writes Arturo; same pencil lead snaps; clock 12:06; 1986 lights begin going out; Tomas's fear becomes undisguised.
15. They promise tomorrow; Daniel tears one page from same notebook and writes exact three-line warning with same broken pencil.
16. Same page crosses backward; fingers brush warm/painless; clock 12:07.
17. Connection closes without visible Tomas dissolution; Daniel is simply alone in abandoned 2026 cafe, window closed.
18. Same warning page returns forward aged forty years with Tomas's added `I will bring better bread.`
19. Daniel preserves same page.
20. Final same-glass reveal below `TOMAS`: `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.`
21. Ending station clock remains exact 12:07.
