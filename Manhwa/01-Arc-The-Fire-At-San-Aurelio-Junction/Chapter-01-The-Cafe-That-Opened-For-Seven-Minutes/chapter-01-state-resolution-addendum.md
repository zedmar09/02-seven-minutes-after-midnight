## 1. Daniel Gloves — Absolute

Daniel puts on thin white cotton conservation gloves before retrieving the token and **does not remove them during the active seven-minute connection**.

Therefore:
- first tray/finger contact includes Daniel's gloved hand
- painful wrist catch is Tomas skin/hand against Daniel through thin cotton where applicable

## 2. Pencil Break — Deterministic Mechanism

At the Arturo note beat, Daniel uses the same ordinary wooden graphite pencil.

The graphite point **chips/snaps**, but a short blunt section of graphite remains exposed and physically usable.

State sequence:
1. Daniel writes `ARTURO SALCEDO`.
2. graphite tip chips/snaps once.
3. pencil remains the same object with shorter/blunter exposed graphite.
4. no sharpener/new pencil appears.
5. at V073 Daniel writes the three-line warning with that same blunt pencil; the line may read slightly heavier/rougher but remains legible.

This resolves the broken-pencil → warning-writing continuity hole.
