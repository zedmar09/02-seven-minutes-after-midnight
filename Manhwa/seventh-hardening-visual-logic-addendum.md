# Seventh Hardening — Visual Logic Addendum

This addendum is binding on all active production under `manhwa-2d-production-auditor`.

It does not replace character/environment/object canon. It adds stricter gates for camera, gaze, physical text, reflections/shadows, grip ergonomics, worn-object attachment, audio geography, and threshold proof.

## Authority

1. current user instruction
2. current chapter/current strip script
3. approved character WebPs
4. approved environment WebPs
5. approved object WebPs
6. current hardened strip prompt
7. previous APPROVED strip temporary state only
8. minimal canon-compatible derived micro-detail only

No camera convenience, reflection, shadow, close-up, or stylization may redefine permanent canon.

## Camera Axis / Eyeline Continuity — Absolute

For one continuous conversation/action:
- preserve each character's world-space side, facing, and distance unless visible movement changes it
- reverse shot may flip screen-left/right but not put Daniel/Tomas on opposite physical sides of counter/window
- eyelines terminate toward real target: other man, clock, window, door scratch, notebook, tray, kitchen audio source
- if crossing 180° axis, first use a neutral/reorientation view
- balloon tails do not substitute for correct eye/head direction

## Physical Text-Plane Lock — Absolute

Applies to:
- clock face
- scratched painted glass
- notebook/torn page/acid-free sleeve
- station/cafe signage

Rules:
- text remains inside/on real carrier boundary
- text perspective follows carrier plane
- a close-up is same physical object, not floating UI
- handwriting follows actual paper surface
- scratch text follows actual damaged paint/glass
- carrier turned away/closed/occluded means text cannot remain floating visible

## Reflection / Shadow False-Duplicate Ban — Absolute

Unless explicitly scripted:
- no readable duplicate Daniel/Tomas/other person in station glass, pastry case, windows, tile, metal, saucer, or clock glass
- no unknown human silhouette/face/hand/shadow-person
- shadows originate only from actual current light sources
- 2026 darkness cannot generate dramatic person-shaped shadows from nonexistent lights
- 1986 cafe bulbs remain ordinary practical sources

## Grip / Finger / Contact Ergonomics — Absolute

Critical handling must be mechanically usable.

For tray, tongs, token, wrist catch, saucer, paper, notebook, pencil, flashlight:
- believable finger count/joint direction
- hand contacts graspable surface
- tongs held by handles
- tray supported from plausible edge/underside
- pencil grip supports writing
- wrist catch is hand-to-wrist, not hand fused through arm
- paper held/supported while writing/passing
- flashlight starts from actual support before fall

## Clothing / Accessory / Hair Attachment — Absolute

- Daniel glasses sit physically on face/ears
- gloves fit hands and do not float/disappear when currently worn
- Tomas hair cord actually gathers hair low at nape/back
- apron ties wrap plausible waist route
- loose strands obey front/behind order and do not pass through faces/hands

## Audio Source / Reaction Direction — Absolute

For 1986 station announcements and Lilia's offscreen kitchen speech:
- establish real source location in story space
- audio lettering points to source without becoming physical object
- relevant head/eye/body reactions turn toward real source
- camera change cannot move source to Daniel's 2026 side or another wall

## Weather / Glass Boundary — Absolute

Rain stays outside intact glass/roof boundaries except where 2026 damage physically allows intrusion. Residual wetness on Daniel/clothing/floor is plausible; active rainfall may not appear inside intact 1986 cafe.

## Threshold / Occlusion Cannot Replace Proof — Absolute

Do not use body, balloon, darkness, steam, hair, counter edge, or crop to avoid drawing:
- tray/finger contact
- wrist catch/release
- service-window opening
- bread/saucer crossing
- paper crossing
- second finger contact

If decisive action is hidden, recompose.

## Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- camera axis silently flips physical sides
- gaze points toward wrong target
- physical text floats off carrier
- reflection/shadow invents duplicate human/object
- critical grip/contact is anatomically unusable
- glasses/gloves/hair cord/apron attachment floats/clips/swaps
- offscreen audio comes from wrong place/era
- rain crosses physical boundary impossibly
- occlusion/crop evades required action proof

These are prompt/preproduction gates. Rendered compliance remains pending until pixels are inspected.
