# Seventh Hardening — Visual Logic Addendum

This addendum is always binding on active Manhwa production unless a later authority makes a rule stricter. It exists to catch physically plausible-looking images that still break story-world logic.

## Camera Axis / Eyeline — Absolute

Within one uninterrupted interaction:
- preserve world-space character sides around the counter/service window
- reverse cameras may flip screen-left/right but not exchange physical positions
- Daniel and Tomas look toward the real speaker/object/sound source
- offscreen Lilia/station audio remains at its physical source and reactions orient accordingly
- crossing the 180° axis requires an understandable reorientation view

## Physical Text Plane — Absolute

In-world text remains on its real carrier and follows perspective/boundaries:
- clock time on clock face
- notes on paper
- `TOMAS` and fire warning on real Cafe Siete glass
- signs on signs/token

Never float evidence as generic UI or let text detach from its carrier.

## Reflection / Shadow False-Duplicate Ban

Unless explicitly scripted, reject:
- reflected duplicate Daniel/Tomas
- human-shaped shadows from nonexistent people
- afterimages/ghost silhouettes mistaken for a second body
- reflected objects that imply an extra physical copy

Ordinary glass/rain may be simplified; it may not invent story entities.

## Grip / Contact Validity

Continuity-critical grips must physically work:
- fingers wrap/contact the tray edge
- Tomas's wrist catch contacts Daniel's actual wrist/forearm
- page transfers use real pinch/support
- tongs grip pastry plausibly
- Daniel's writing hand contacts pencil/page

Flat 2D overlap alone is not proof of contact.

## Worn / Attached Items

Glasses, apron, hair cord, clothing, straps, and any future bag/watch/accessory must physically attach to the correct body location without clipping/floating or side swapping.

## Audio Source Logic

Offscreen station announcements, Lilia's voice, gate sounds, clock click/tick, pencil snap, and flashlight impact remain tied to real story-world sources. Do not place visual sound cues on the wrong era/object.

## Weather / Glass Boundary

Rain stays outside intact glass/roof surfaces and enters only through established damage/openings. 1986 intact surfaces cannot leak 2026 roof damage into Tomas's era without explicit story cause.

## Dual-Era Boundary Logic

The same cafe structure can show different historical states, but:
- Daniel-side 2026 damage remains on Daniel's state
- Tomas-side 1986 operation remains on Tomas's state
- shared threshold objects cross only through scripted mechanics
- no random 1986 passenger or 2026 construction object appears on the wrong side

## Service-Window Mechanics

Preserve opening direction/track/hinge/mechanism once canonicalized. Do not change a sliding window into a hinged hatch or move handles/tracks for composition.

## Clock Mechanics

The recurring clock keeps one construction. Camera reversal may not mirror numerals/hands into impossible orientation. Time must match the script exactly.

## Object Provenance / Topology

Every plot object has one continuous history. Track count, location, orientation, era, age state, and support. Close-up is same object unless script explicitly establishes another.

## Route / Collision Logic

Daniel's movement through station/cafe, reaching under counter, turning toward glass, retrieving/using flashlight, and handling evidence must avoid impossible teleporting through furniture/walls/objects.

## Approval Gate

Any visually attractive render that violates these rules is a blocking failure and must be repaired/regenerated before sequential production continues.
