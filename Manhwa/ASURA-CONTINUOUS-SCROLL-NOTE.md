# Continuous Reader Reference Note

The reference reader behavior is **one continuous vertical chapter**, even when a publishing site technically delivers that chapter through multiple same-width image files.

## Production Principle

- continuous top-to-bottom reading
- fixed-width sequential images
- no reader-visible page breaks between upload images
- some technical image boundaries may cut through a continuing background/composition when seam continuity remains exact
- varied vertical negative space only when it performs a real pacing/story function
- full-width or tall reveal art where useful
- mobile-readable balloons and physical evidence
- SFX integrated into art rather than isolated in printed-page margins

## What We Do Not Copy

Do not copy another series' exact drawings, characters, costumes, poses, backgrounds, panel layouts, dialogue, effects, typography assets, logos, or watermarks.

## Chapter 1 Interpretation

Chapter 1 is one continuous reader experience planned as **74 internal read beats (`V001–V074`) and 16 technical strips (`strip-001–strip-016`)**.

Technical files exist only because production is safer and more controllable sequentially than as one enormous generated image. Strip boundaries are invisible in the final stitch.

When a composition naturally crosses a technical boundary, continue the same scene/art/state using `seam-continuity-protocol.md`.

Negative space must remain purposeful and obey the no-dead-space gate. Do not create giant blank bands merely to imitate another site's scrolling rhythm.

All output remains subject to `style-guide.md` and all hardening addenda, including the strict flat 2D human-drawn / no-gloss / no-cinematic gate.
