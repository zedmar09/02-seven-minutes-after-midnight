# Manhwa Reference Vibe Profile

## Target Reading Experience

Create the feeling of a polished contemporary Korean vertical webtoon/manhwa release without copying another title's exact artwork.

**Polished means clean production discipline, stable canon, readable composition, intentional linework, controlled lettering, and convincing continuity — never glossy, cinematic, photorealistic, 3D, painterly, airbrushed, or over-rendered.**

`style-guide.md` is the absolute rendering authority.

The reader should feel there are no traditional pages. They simply scroll through one continuous chapter.

## Story-Specific Vibe

`Seven Minutes After Midnight` should feel:
- intimate
- adult
- rain-soaked without glossy cinema
- archival and tactile
- warm in 1986 / cold in 2026 through flat value/color design
- emotionally restrained
- increasingly time-pressured
- grounded in real railway/cafe architecture
- mysterious because evidence disagrees, not because random supernatural decorations appear

The central visual motif is the narrow service window separating two living adults by forty years.

## Rhythm

Use a mixture of:
- large station/cafe establishing images
- medium Daniel/Tomas conversation views
- close reaction shots
- narrow clock/evidence/handwriting inserts
- borderless/tall service-window reveals
- short object-transformation sequences
- meaningful negative space before clock/reveal/contact beats
- environmental continuation through rain/steam/darkness when it performs a story function

Do not mechanically convert old page prompts or one prose paragraph into one panel.

## Visual Density

Archive/investigation setup may carry multiple compact beats.

Connection/reveal/romance/time-pressure beats may expand:
- first Tomas reveal should breathe
- first accidental touch should be readable
- service-window opening deserves clear spatial proof
- aged pastry transformation should read as one object changing
- each clock-minute transition may use different emphasis depending on story pressure
- 12:06→12:07 should lengthen perceived time without filler
- returned note and final scratch warning should each land clearly

## Cross-Image Continuity

Technical strip edge is not story edge.

A station wall, counter, service window, rain field, 1986 warm-cafe field, 2026 darkness, or character composition may continue into the next technical strip. Final stitched chapter must look designed on one continuous canvas.

## Romance Presentation

Romance reads through:
- gaze and attention
- hands working/passing objects
- proximity at the service window
- work-specific care
- private jokes
- a line becoming unexpectedly vulnerable
- reluctance to end the meeting

Avoid sparkle effects, glamour close-ups, generic blush overlays, chibi reactions, possessive melodrama, or cinematic erotic framing not required by the story.

## Mystery Presentation

Mystery reads through physical evidence:
- cut archive names
- stopped clock
- scratch marks
- wrong-age objects
- signal clue
- Arturo's presence
- smoke stain
- changed handwriting

Do not replace evidence with floating UI, supernatural glyphs, ghost silhouettes, or abstract lore graphics.
