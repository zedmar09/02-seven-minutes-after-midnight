# Reference Vibe Profile — Seven Minutes After Midnight

This file defines story-specific visual atmosphere beneath the absolute `style-guide.md`. It may never loosen the flat-2D production gate.

## Emotional Target

Adult BL time-slip romance under historical-mystery pressure: quiet attraction, procedural curiosity, midnight intimacy, archival dread, railway melancholy, and the fear that every attempt to save someone may erase the place where love became possible.

## Visual Motifs

- brass clock and seven-minute countdown
- narrow service window
- hands meeting across a threshold
- flour on Tomas's sleeves/forearms
- Daniel's careful conservation handling
- handwritten notes and acid-free sleeves
- objects aging forty years
- scratched names/warnings on old glass
- rain on abandoned station surfaces
- intact 1986 cafe versus damaged 2026 cafe
- pastry steam versus archive dust
- scorch evidence and missing records

## Color / Material Direction

Keep colors grounded, matte, and local rather than cinematic.

1986 may use warmer practical cafe colors, jade hair-cord accent, cream/brown workwear, brass, tile greens/creams, food colors, and ordinary station lighting.

2026 may use cooler dusty neutrals, weathered tile, oxidized brass, paper creams, muted chore-jacket blue, plywood/rust/rain values, and practical flashlight/light-source logic.

Do not turn this into orange/teal grading, blue-night glamour, neon time portals, bloom, glossy rain reflections, or photoreal railway noir.

## Romance Framing

Prefer eye-line exchanges, interrupted jokes, hands near/touching, shared objects, restrained close-ups, and vertical breathing room around emotionally important moments. Avoid fanservice posing, school-romance shorthand, chibi reaction comedy, glamour lighting, or melodramatic kiss framing not present in the script.

## Mystery Framing

Use readable evidence: documents, cut newspaper strips, token, clock, scratched glass, signal/fire clues, and physical object transformations. Suspense should come from causal discovery and pacing, not vague shadow people, horror faces, magical smoke, or cinematic lens tricks.

## Time-Slip Framing

The supernatural feels physically embedded in one real historical building. The same counter/window/clock relationships persist. The impossible is the coexistence of two historical states and object/time behavior—not a floating portal graphic.

## Hard Ban

No photorealism, semi-realism, CGI, glossy surfaces, excessive reflections, depth-of-field blur, bloom, lens flare, over-rendered hair/skin, painterly concept-art look, or cinematic color treatment.
