# Eighth Hardening — Presentation Integrity Addendum

This addendum extends the active `manhwa-2d-production-auditor` rules. It closes loopholes where correct story objects/actions technically exist but are warped, cropped, too small, poorly lit, or ordered so badly that the reader cannot verify the event.

## Perspective / Lens Integrity — Absolute

Reject:
- fisheye/ultra-wide distortion of station/cafe straight architecture
- extreme perspective that changes hand/object/body world proportions
- dutch/oblique framing that makes counter/window/floor support ambiguous
- forced perspective that fakes threshold contact
- close-up perspective that reverses service-window, hand, paper, clock, or body orientation

Use ordinary manhwa perspective. Drama comes from framing and pacing, not lens warping.

## Mobile-Scale Evidence Legibility — Absolute

The following cannot be technically present but microscopic:
- `TOMAS` scratch
- token `7`
- clock time when it is a story beat
- first tray/finger contact
- 12:02 wrist catch
- service-window opening
- bread/saucer crossing and aging
- acid-free sleeve text/crossing
- apron-pocket placement
- flashlight fall/result
- Arturo name/pencil snap
- three-line warning
- returned aged page/Tomas addition
- final fire scratch

If wide framing is insufficient, add immediately adjacent SAME-OBJECT detail while preserving state.

## Crop / Gutter / Divider Safe Margin — Absolute

Keep decisive contact and enough context fully inside image area. Do not place critical hands, paper edges, tray/tongs, clock, scratch text, service-window opening, or object transformation exactly on crop/gutter/seam/divider.

## Lighting Source / Shadow Direction — Absolute

Within same lighting state, preserve physical source direction.

- 2026 abandoned: only plausible ambient/flashlight/source state
- 1986 active cafe: practical warm cafe bulbs, flat matte treatment
- shared connection: each era remains physically plausible
- 12:06: scripted 1986 lights extinguish sequentially
- 12:07: return to established 2026 state

No screen-like portal glow, cinematic rim light, or arbitrary shadow-side swaps.

## Top-to-Bottom Reader Order — Absolute

Action and text read in exact causal order.

- clock insert belongs at correct minute transition
- later dialogue cannot visually precede earlier dialogue
- physical writing cannot appear before writing action
- aged result cannot appear before threshold crossing
- Lilia audio must occur after flashlight impact as locked
- Arturo clue precedes 12:06 collapse
- returned note precedes final scratch reveal

If order is ambiguous, split into more vertical slices rather than rewriting dialogue.

## Critical Object / Limb Silhouette Completeness — Absolute

Close-ups show enough connected context to prove ownership/orientation:
- wrist catch belongs to Tomas/Daniel correctly
- tongs belong to Tomas's same hand/body
- paper belongs to same notebook/sleeve/page
- aged bread/saucer are same crossed objects
- scratch belongs to same painted glass

## Readable In-World Text — Absolute

Exact story text must remain readable at mobile size. Do not corrupt it into decorative pseudo-text.

## Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- perspective warps canon
- critical proof is too small
- crop/gutter/seam amputates decisive evidence
- lighting/source direction changes without cause
- reader order contradicts script
- ownership becomes ambiguous in close-up
- required physical text is unreadable or decorative gibberish

Rendered compliance remains pending until pixels are inspected.
