# Eighth Hardening — Presentation Integrity Addendum

This addendum is always binding on active Manhwa production and catches failures that are technically present but unreadable, cropped, reordered, or distorted in presentation.

## Perspective / Lens Integrity — Absolute

Use ordinary human-eye/manhwa perspective. Reject fisheye, ultra-wide curvature, forced foreground scaling, warped straight architecture, or perspective that changes canonical body/object proportions.

Drama comes from framing, shot size, negative space, vertical pacing, and acting—not lens distortion.

## Mobile-Scale Proof — Absolute

Continuity-critical evidence must remain understandable at normal phone reading size. It is not enough for proof to exist as a few tiny pixels.

Chapter 1 mobile-proof priorities:
- stopped/active clock times
- `TOMAS` scratch
- token markings
- same-tray hand contact
- Daniel wrist/Tomas grip ownership
- service-window opening
- pastry/saucer threshold crossing and aging
- `I AM REAL.` sheet text and transfer
- Tomas apron-pocket storage
- Arturo name/pencil snap
- warning-page text and transfer
- returned page aging + Tomas reply
- final fire-warning scratch

Use immediately adjacent same-object details rather than resizing the physical object unrealistically.

## Crop / Gutter / Divider Safe Margin

Do not place decisive hand contact, note edges, token, clock, service-window mechanism, scratch text, or aging transformation exactly on canvas crops/gutters/diagonal dividers/technical seams. Single-pixel survival does not count as readable proof.

## Lighting Source / Shadow Integrity

Within one unchanged state, preserve practical light-source direction. 1986 practical lights can progressively fail; 2026 flashlight acts only where physically present. The time-slip itself is not permission for decorative rim light, glow, or cinematic shadow reversal.

## Reader Causal Order — Absolute

Top-to-bottom order must match script causality:
- year exchange before 12:01 reaction
- service window opens before pastry uses it
- object crosses before aging result is read
- Daniel writes before Tomas reads/pockets
- Arturo clue before 12:06 fear beat
- warning page transfers before 12:07 closure
- closure before returned aged page
- returned page before final fire-warning discovery

If one composition makes order ambiguous, split into more vertical slices instead of rewriting causality.

## Close-Up Ownership Context

Critical close-ups retain enough adjacent context to prove which body/object they belong to. A hand, note, token, pastry, or clock detail may be magnified on page but cannot become an ownerless generic insert.

## Text Readability

Exact story-critical wording remains legible and attached to carrier. Do not hide text under perspective, flour, reflections, hands, damage, SFX, or stylized distortion.

## Era Readability

When both eras appear together, the reader must understand which historical state belongs to Daniel and which to Tomas without relying on a colored portal stripe or production label. Use environment condition, clothing, practical lighting, occupancy, and material age.

## Dead-Space / Seam Integrity

Large vertical gaps need real story function. Reject purposeless double gaps at strip joins, giant black filler bands, repeated empty bottoms, or technical seam spacing that becomes visible after stitching.

## Final Gate

A strip that contains correct story facts but presents them at unreadable scale, wrong order, bad crop, warped perspective, or ambiguous ownership fails visual QA.
