# San Aurelio Municipal Museum Archive / Conservation Workroom Bible

## Core Identity

A functioning present-day paper/photograph conservation workroom inside the **fictional San Aurelio Municipal Museum** in fictional Caligo Vale, used for the San Aurelio Railway Archive restoration project.

Professional, modest, organized and physically believable—not a grand library, police evidence room, futuristic lab, luxury office or horror archive.

## Fictional Institution Rule — Absolute

No real museum/archive/city/national identity may leak into the room.

Do not use real flags/maps/seals, national/city archive branding, transit logos, recognizable real institutional interiors or readable real government forms/signage.
