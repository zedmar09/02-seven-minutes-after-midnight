# San Aurelio Junction Environment Pack

San Aurelio Junction is a **fictional** historical railway station in the fictional municipality of San Aurelio, inside fictional Caligo Vale. It is shown in 1986 and 2026.

## No Real-Place Substitution — Absolute

Do not base the station one-to-one on a real station, famous terminal, real city or real national railway system.

Do not include:
- real flags/maps
- real transit logos or route maps
- real government/civic seals
- famous skyline/landmark views
- recognizable real station names/signage

The architecture below is San Aurelio's own canon.

## Permanent Structural Identity

The approved canonical pack must lock:
- ticket-hall footprint
- Cafe Siete location beside the ticket hall
- arched window pattern
- green-and-cream tile relationships
- ticket-window zones
- major platform/ticket-hall circulation
- station-clock placement where story-critical
- iron structural members
- brass signage language
- key doors/service routes relevant to the fire mystery

## 1986 State

Operational fictional railway station with intact tile/fixtures, passengers/staff, announcements, practical period lighting, working fictional signs, and ordinary railway activity.

## 2026 State

Abandoned/redevelopment state with dust, broken tile, plywood, rust, cracked glass, caution/redevelopment material, archive/conservation access, rain damage, and unoccupied dark zones.
