# San Aurelio Junction — Dual-Era Environment Bible

## Structural Identity — Permanent

San Aurelio Junction is one physical railway station seen forty years apart.

Permanent architecture must be coordinated through a single geometry authority:
- main ticket hall
- Cafe Siete location beside ticket hall
- primary station doors and arched-window rhythm
- main concourse circulation
- platform access direction
- service-corridor relationship when later canonicalized
- structural columns/iron framing
- key clock/signage anchors

Do not independently compose a 1986 station and a 2026 station that only share colors.

## 1986 State

- station operational and busy
- intact tile/fixtures/windows
- working station announcements
- passenger silhouettes/circulation where story allows
- active practical lighting
- functional ticket/cafe/signage areas
- wet coats/rain visible at exterior boundaries when weather applies
- ordinary 1980s wear, not pristine fantasy nostalgia

## 2026 State

- abandoned/partly cordoned for redevelopment
- dust, rust, broken tile, plaster damage
- plywood/barriers/caution/redevelopment material where plausible
- rain intrusion only through actual damage/openings
- dark inactive ticket/service areas
- archival investigation access
- stopped/abandoned station-clock language

## Cross-Era Conservation

Allowed differences:
- removable furniture
- equipment modernization/removal
- condition/damage/scorch
- dirt/rust/paint loss
- signage replacement
- crowd/occupancy
- lighting
- redevelopment barriers
- documented physical renovation

Not allowed without explicit story canon:
- moving Cafe Siete to another wall
- changing station footprint
- moving major windows/doors
- mirroring platform direction
- changing structural column/grid logic
- inventing a second incompatible ticket hall

## Visual Style

Strict flat 2D human-drawn full-color manhwa. Matte tile, brass, paint, glass, iron, paper and rain treatment. No glossy cinematic wet floor, photoreal architecture, CGI, bloom, depth-of-field blur, or dramatic lens distortion.

## Camera Rule

Every reverse angle must remain physically derivable from the floor-plan/angle-atlas authority. If desired shot would require impossible architecture, change the shot.
