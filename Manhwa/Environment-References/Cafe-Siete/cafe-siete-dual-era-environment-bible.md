# Cafe Siete — Dual-Era Environment Bible

## Permanent Structure — Absolute

Cafe Siete is one physical cafe architecture existing in both 1986 and 2026.

Lock through approved floor plan/reference pack:
- footprint within San Aurelio Junction
- cafe entry/glass wall relationship
- counter footprint/height/depth
- narrow service-window location and opening size
- kitchen side vs customer/counter side
- clock/menu-wall location
- major windows/doors
- pastry-case zone
- physically valid walking/reaching routes

## 1986 Operational State

- intact cafe surfaces
- active practical bulbs
- functioning ceiling fan where established
- pastry case stocked
- tray/saucer/tongs/work objects available
- handwritten menu board
- coffee steam/flour activity
- station staff/passenger audio beyond cafe
- service-window frame clean/intact
- glass clear enough for ordinary station reflections without readable duplicate humans unless physically present

## 2026 Abandoned State

- dust, rust, old sugar smell implied through visual condition
- damaged/missing stools/furniture
- cracked/empty pastry case
- ghost chalk on menu board
- painted/aged cafe glass
- old `TOMAS` scratch in painted glass
- second fire-warning scratch hidden/revealed later on same glass
- service-window lower edge charred as Chapter 1 establishes
- broken plaster/debris where canon allows
- no live cafe staff/food inventory

## Shared-Connection State

During the seven-minute connection, Daniel's 2026 side and Tomas's 1986 side may coexist visually around the same counter/threshold.

Important:
- do not merge the two eras into one decorative half-blue/half-orange fantasy room unless the actual geometry remains clear
- the counter may become a shared physical surface under story rules
- the service-window opening is the reliable small-object exchange point
- each side retains its own plausible environment state/light
- body/object crossing depth must remain readable

## Service-Window Size — Absolute

Small enough to deny full-body crossing. Large enough for:
- saucer/roll
- paper/sleeve
- hands/limited forearm reach

Never enlarge it for a two-shot, embrace, or dramatic full-body portal composition.

## Clock Relationship

The Cafe Siete brass-rimmed clock must remain in one stable wall relationship. When shown at multiple angles, preserve physical location and face orientation. Chapter 1 time is exact.

## Painted Glass / Scratch Relationship

`TOMAS` and the later `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.` are on Daniel's 2026 painted-glass state. Tomas's 1986 side has clear/intact glass and he states he did not write the first scratch.

Do not accidentally show the old scratches on Tomas's 1986 glass.

## Style

Strict flat 2D human-drawn full-color Korean manhwa/webtoon. Matte wood/tile/brass/glass/paper/ceramic. No photorealism, 3D, cinematic bloom, glossy wet surfaces, depth-of-field blur, or painterly nostalgia.
