# Cafe Siete Environment Pack

Cafe Siete is the central recurring environment and time-slip anchor.

## Structural Locks

The approved pack must establish one shared physical cafe structure across both eras:
- counter footprint and height
- service-window location, width, sill/depth, frame, and opening mechanics
- clock/menu-wall relationship
- kitchen/counter relationship
- cafe entrance/gate relationship
- pastry-case zone
- fixed wall/opening geometry
- physically plausible Daniel/Tomas sightlines and hand-contact positions

## 1986 State

Operational railway cafe:
- intact finishes
- active pastry case
- working practical bulbs
- ceiling fan where established
- handwritten menu use
- active kitchen/station sound/traffic
- period-correct equipment and removable furniture

## 2026 State

Abandoned cafe:
- dust and age
- broken/stripped furnishings where established
- damaged/painted glass
- charred service-window evidence
- fallen plaster / deterioration
- redevelopment-era intrusion outside the cafe where relevant
- no accidental active 1986 fixtures after the connection closes

## Required Canonical Outputs Before Chapter 1 Rendering

At minimum approve WebPs for:
1. shared structural/floor-layout authority
2. 2026 cafe wide
3. 1986 cafe wide
4. counter + service-window geometry authority
5. clock/menu-wall authority
6. dual-era state sheet or compatible paired reference proving one structure
7. useful reverse-angle/angle-atlas authority

Exact filenames should be committed and then referenced by Chapter 1 production Markdown before Strip 001 generation.
