# Cafe Siete Environment Bible

## Core Identity

Cafe Siete is a compact **fictional** railway cafe beside the old ticket hall inside fictional San Aurelio Junction, in fictional Caligo Vale. It is not a renamed real cafe, fantasy tavern, palace tea room, glossy modern coffee shop, or supernatural train lounge.

The same permanent cafe shell exists in 1986 and 2026.

## Fictional Visual Identity — Absolute

Do not copy a real cafe/station concession or import real-country/city identity.

No real:
- flag/map/civic seal
- railway/transit logo
- cafe chain logo
- famous station/landmark geometry
- readable real place names
- real government/municipal branding

Any signage/menu/fixture identity must be fictional San Aurelio design.

## Permanent Geometry

Until approved WebPs establish exact measured geometry, generation remains blocked. The canonical pack must ultimately lock:

- pastry-case zone
- cafe glass/sign zone where `TOMAS` and later fire warning are scratched in 2026
- floor/wall relationships and circulation space

## 1986 Material / State Vocabulary

- intact green-and-cream station/cafe tile relationships where visible
- painted/plastered walls appropriate to working fictional railway cafe
- matte brass fixtures/signage

- ordinary glass
- pastry case stocked

- dust, grime, oxidation, worn paint

- no glossy abandoned-building reflections
