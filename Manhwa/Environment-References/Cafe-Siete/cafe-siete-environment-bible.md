# Cafe Siete Environment Bible

## Core Identity

Cafe Siete is a compact railway cafe beside the old ticket hall inside San Aurelio Junction. It is not a fantasy tavern, palace tea room, glossy modern coffee shop, or supernatural train lounge.

The same permanent cafe shell must exist in 1986 and 2026.

## Permanent Geometry

Until approved WebPs establish exact measured geometry, all generation remains blocked. The canonical pack must ultimately lock:
- cafe entrance/gate side relative to counter
- main counter length/orientation
- service-window opening behind/within the counter-kitchen relationship
- kitchen beyond service window
- brass-rimmed wall clock location relative to menu board and counter
- pastry-case zone
- cafe glass/sign zone where `TOMAS` and the later fire warning are scratched in 2026
- floor/wall relationships and circulation space

Do not infer arbitrary alternate layouts from individual legacy page prompts.

## 1986 Material / State Vocabulary

- intact green-and-cream station/cafe tile relationships where visible
- painted/plastered walls appropriate to working railway cafe
- matte brass fixtures/signage
- matte wood/laminate counter surfaces
- ordinary glass
- pastry case stocked
- paper menus / handwritten board
- practical warm bulbs represented with flat color/value
- working ceiling fan where established
- ordinary steam/flour/coffee atmosphere without cinematic haze

## 2026 Material / State Vocabulary

- same structural shell, aged forty years
- dust, grime, oxidation, worn paint
- broken or missing removable furniture
- cracked/painted/dirty glass where established
- char along service-window lower edge where story establishes it
- fallen plaster / redevelopment neglect
- no glossy abandoned-building reflections

## Time-Slip Activation

At 12:00 Chapter 1 presents an impossible simultaneous relationship: Daniel remains anchored to the 2026 side/state while Tomas and active 1986 cafe life become accessible across the shared counter/window logic. The art must make the historical disagreement readable without turning the opening into a magical portal.

## Contact Geometry

Daniel and Tomas must be able to:
- catch opposite edges of the same tray/counter interaction
- place hands on shared counter
- reach through the service window for plates/notes/hand contact

The window is not large enough for a torso/body crossing.

## Final Evidence Geometry

After 12:07 the cafe is fully 2026 again. The returned aged page lands on the counter. Daniel can then visually discover the additional scratch `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.` on the same Cafe Siete glass/sign zone that already carried `TOMAS`.
