# Environment References

Recurring locations use approved canonical environment WebPs as permanent geometry/state authority.

## Fictional Geography Policy — Absolute

All story environments belong to the fictional Caligo Vale / San Aurelio world defined by `../Continuity/fictional-setting-bible.md`.

Environment generation must not use or reproduce:
- real country/city/province/state names
- real flags, coats of arms, maps or civic seals
- real railway/transit logos or agency branding
- famous skylines or landmark replicas
- recognizable one-to-one copies of real railway stations, museums or cafes
- real license plates, postal codes, phone formats or government signage

`San Aurelio Junction`, `San Aurelio Municipal Museum` and `Cafe Siete` are fictional canon. Their architecture must be designed as internal story-world identity, not sourced as a renamed real place.

## Policy

- Exact committed `.webp` files are production authority; Markdown prompts/bibles are not substitutes.
- Preserve structural footprint, doors/openings, fixed architectural relationships, counter/service-window geometry, clock/menu-wall relationship, routes, scale, and physically possible reverse angles.
- 1986 and 2026 are historical states of the same San Aurelio Junction / Cafe Siete structure.
- Era-specific condition, furnishings, occupancy, weather exposure, damage, practical lighting and clutter may differ where canon allows.
- Missing required approved environment WebP is blocking before strip generation.
