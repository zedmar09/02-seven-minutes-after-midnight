# Vertical Scroll Layout Guide

## One Chapter, One Scroll

The finished chapter is one centered continuous vertical reader. Technical strips are invisible production chunks only.

No traditional comic pages, reader-visible page numbers, strip numbers, panel numbers, beat IDs, scene labels, shot labels, QA notes, headers, or technical footers.

## Width Lock

Use one fixed generation width for every technical strip in a chapter. Do not independently resize individual strips before stitching. Stitch approved strips first, then uniformly resize/export the completed master if needed.

## Canonical Environment Anchor — Absolute

Every camera view must be physically possible inside approved environment canon.

Preserve:
- San Aurelio Junction footprint/routes
- Cafe Siete position relative to ticket hall
- cafe counter/service-window geometry
- doors/windows/major fixed openings
- clock/menu-wall relationship
- recurring furniture/fixture anchors when canonicalized
- reverse-angle truth
- world-space scale

Never mirror or relocate fixed architecture for composition convenience. Recompose the camera.

## Dual-Era Geometry — Absolute

A 1986 view and a 2026 view may differ in state but must derive from the same underlying architecture.

When one composition compares both eras, preserve matching structural anchors so the reader can understand that the same place is disagreeing in time.

Allowed era differences include occupancy, condition, age, scorch, dirt, furniture removal/replacement, lighting, signage, redevelopment barriers, intact/broken finishes, and documented renovation.

## Read Beats Are Not Reader Labels

Internal `V###` IDs and temporary-state names are planning only. Never render `V`, `P`, `strip`, `beat`, `panel`, `scene`, `shot`, `QA`, era-state labels, clock-state labels, or continuity-ledger names in artwork.

## Adjacent-Slice Micro-Continuity — Absolute

New slice = new camera/read beat, **not reset**.

Preserve all unmodified state including:
- body position/facing/feet/distance
- hand occupancy and anatomical side
- clothing-layer/sleeve state
- hair tie/loose-strand identity
- glasses/gloves/accessories
- object count/location/orientation/scale/depth/support
- token/notebook/pencil/flashlight/tray/tongs/saucer/bread/paper state
- service-window state
- door/glass/counter state
- era state
- clock minute
- station/cafe light state
- camera-axis geography
- emotional intensity

Occlusion is not disappearance or relocation.

## Temporary Anatomical-Side Continuity

Use production-only identities when reverse-shot drift could break a critical action, for example:
- `TOKEN HAND`
- `FLASHLIGHT HAND`
- `NOTEBOOK HAND`
- `PENCIL HAND`
- `TRAY HAND`
- `TONGS HAND`
- `WINDOW HAND`
- `WRIST-CATCH HAND`

Screen-left/right may reverse; anatomical side may not. Never render these labels.

## Camera Axis / Eyeline Continuity — Absolute

For the same uninterrupted conversation/action:
- preserve world-space sides and distances
- Daniel looks toward the actual Tomas/service-window/clock/door/object position
- Tomas looks toward actual Daniel/window/clock/kitchen/audio source
- reverse shot may flip screen-left/right but not physical side
- crossing the 180° axis requires a neutral/reorientation view
- a close-up cannot hide an eyeline that contradicts canonical geography

## Perspective / Lens Integrity — Absolute

Use ordinary human-eye/manhwa perspective.

Reject:
- fisheye/ultra-wide curvature of station/cafe straight lines
- forced perspective that changes body/object world proportions
- extreme hand/object enlargement that breaks connection to body
- perspective that fakes touch across the service window
- framing that makes floor support, counter depth, window threshold, or object crossing ambiguous

Drama comes from shot size, framing, vertical pacing, and negative space—not lens warping.

## Mobile-Scale Evidence Legibility — Absolute

Story-critical proof must be understandable at normal phone size.

The following cannot be microscopic/ambiguous:
- `TOMAS` scratched on glass
- brass token `7` identity when first established
- clock minute changes
- first tray/finger contact
- Daniel/Tomas wrist-contact ownership at 12:02
- service-window opening
- bread/saucer crossing and aging
- acid-free sleeve handwriting and crossing
- apron-pocket placement
- flashlight fall
- Arturo name/notebook/pencil break
- three-line warning page
- second warm finger contact
- returned aged page and Tomas's added line
- `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.`

If a wide is insufficient, use an immediately adjacent SAME-OBJECT detail with enough context. Never enlarge the story-world object itself.

## Crop / Gutter / Divider Safe Margin — Absolute

Continuity-critical proof gets compact breathing room from image edges.

Do not let crop/gutter/divider/seam amputate:
- hands at threshold
- tray/tongs/saucer/bread
- token
- clock hands/numerals
- notebook/page/pencil
- service-window frame/opening
- scratched text carrier
- apron pocket transfer

Single-pixel inclusion does not count as proof.

## Depth / Contact Continuity — Absolute

Flat overlap is not physical contact.

For threshold/prop contact, show enough contour and depth to prove:
- both hands actually stabilize the tray
- Tomas actually catches Daniel's wrist
- tongs grip/place the roll
- saucer physically crosses the window
- Daniel physically hands paper through the opening
- Tomas physically catches and pockets it
- fingers actually brush at the second contact

## Flexible Object / Gravity / No-Clipping — Absolute

Hair, apron ties, paper, notebook pages, coat fabric, bag straps, cord-like hair tie, falling flashlight, pastry crumbs, and loose objects obey support/gravity/collision.

Reject floating paper, impossible falling paths, hair through faces/hands, apron ties through body, or flashlight teleport.

## Clock State / Reader Order — Absolute

The vertical scroll reads in exact causal sequence:

`12:00 → 12:01 → 12:02 → 12:03 → 12:04 → 12:05 → 12:06 → 12:07`

A clock insert may not appear visually below later dialogue if it belongs before that dialogue. Later minute text may not be accidentally read before earlier events.

## Time-Slip Transformation Layout — Absolute

When showing one object before/after time crossing, make SAME-object semantics unmistakable.

A wide plus aged close-up is not two breads/saucers/pages. Use ordered slices/dividers and visible continuity so the reader understands one object transforming.

Never place young and aged forms in a composition that reads as two physical copies unless it is explicitly a representational before/after detail with clear same-object semantics.

## Physical Text Plane

Scratched warnings stay on glass. Handwriting stays on actual paper. Clock time stays on clock. Station signage stays on real sign surfaces.

Representational close-up may enlarge the same carrier, but text never becomes floating UI.

## Lighting Source Continuity

Within unchanged state, keep physically coherent lighting.

### 2026 Abandoned

Use restrained cold ambient/flashlight/practical light actually available. No decorative rim lights.

### 1986 Active Cafe

Use ordinary warm practical cafe lighting with flat matte values; no bloom-heavy amber cinema.

### Shared Connection

Both era states may disagree spatially but each still has plausible real light sources.

### 12:06 Collapse

1986 lights go out in scripted order. Do not invent lightning or portal beams.

### 12:07 Close

Return to established 2026 abandoned baseline.

## Reflection / Shadow False-Duplicate Ban

Unless scripted, no readable reflected duplicate Daniel/Tomas/other human or evidence object in station glass, pastry case, windows, tile, or metal. Cast shadows come only from actual light sources.

## Top-to-Bottom Dialogue / SFX / Audio Order

Reader-facing text and action follow exact script order.

- station announcement is anchored to 1986 audio geography
- Lilia's offscreen kitchen line is visually sourced from the kitchen direction
- dialogue tails never cross/ambiguous ownership
- `click`, impact, flicker, or other SFX sits at physical cause
- handwriting cannot be read before it is written/revealed

If one composition cannot preserve order, use more vertical space or additional unlabeled slices. Do not rewrite story wording to solve layout.

## Gutter Grammar

Every distinct vertical read slice gets a compact black gutter or clearly intentional separation. Side-by-side views may use a diagonal/slanted black divider where useful.

Do not use giant filler bands. Suspense gaps may be larger only when they perform a real pacing function.

## Narration Placement

Narration/captions follow scenario, not one repeated top-left box. Do not cover action, faces, threshold contact, clock, notes, or environment anchors.

## No Dead Bottom Canvas — Absolute

No huge empty lower area unless it performs real story pacing. Never invent filler dialogue/SFX/narration/ghost effects.

## Seam Safety

Reader-facing text and decisive physical action must not be split across a technical seam in a way that breaks comprehension. A continuing environment/effect may cross seams only when the final stitched join is invisible.
