# Ninth Hardening — Character Topology / Identity Addendum

This addendum is always binding on active Manhwa production.

## Whole-Body Anatomical Topology — Absolute

Every visible character is one coherent human body.

Reject:
- extra or missing arms/hands/legs/feet/ears/fingers
- duplicated limbs/body parts
- detached hands/feet/hair masses
- impossible elbow/knee/wrist/ankle/shoulder/hip articulation
- hand/arm emerging from wrong side of torso
- close-up limb that cannot connect to adjacent body pose
- clothing used to hide impossible topology

Partial occlusion is allowed only when hidden connection remains physically possible.

## Limb Ownership / Reverse Shot — Absolute

When a continuity-critical hand/wrist/arm/foot appears:
- it belongs to the SAME character
- it remains the SAME anatomical side established by action
- sleeve/glove/skin/accessory cues agree
- screen-left/right may reverse; anatomical ownership may not

Chapter 1 high-risk temporary identities include Daniel's unstable-contact LEFT HAND, WRITING HAND, TOKEN HAND, FLASHLIGHT HAND, Tomas's WRIST-CATCH HAND, TRAY HAND, TONGS HAND, NOTE HAND, and the apron pocket containing the archival sheet.

## Daniel Permanent Identity

- exactly age 36 in 2026
- same long narrow adult face
- same warm brown skin
- same small healed scar across nose bridge
- same close-cropped black curls with early silver at temples
- same slim medium-tall build
- same rectangular work-glasses construction when worn

Reject teenage/doll-like recasting, different scar, wrong hair length/part, beard/stubble invention, glamour-idol face, broad superhero build, or corporate/fantasy redesign.

## Tomas Permanent Identity

- exactly age 28 in 1986
- same tan skin / bright dark eyes / strong brows
- same lean baker build
- same long dark wavy hair past shoulders
- same LOW practical tie with thin jade-green cord when working
- same cream cafe shirt / brown waist apron construction

Reject short hair, high ponytail, bun, aristocratic half-up hair, xianxia/ghost-prince styling, idol face, schoolboy age drift, or bodybuilder recast.

## Hair Motion vs Redesign

Hair may move from leaning, turning, steam, or gravity. It may not change length, wave family, tie height, cord color, or silhouette identity because camera changes.

## Wardrobe / Local Color / Material

Within continuous Chapter 1 interval, Daniel's chore jacket/glasses/trousers and Tomas's cafe shirt/apron/hair cord remain stable. Lighting may change value, not garment identity/color/material.

Character materials remain matte. Reject glossy close-up skin, wet hair without physical cause, plastic apron/jacket, glowing glasses, or beauty-ad specular treatment.

## Clothing / Body Occlusion

Sleeves, apron, jacket, hair, glasses, and any props must attach correctly to one body. Reject clothing folds implying hidden extra limbs, hands emerging without arms, hair cutting through shoulders, or apron strings/fabric clipping through torso.

## Speaking / Listening / Mouth-State

Visible acting must agree with dialogue ownership. Lilia is offscreen voice in current Chapter 1 and must not be given an unapproved visible mouth/body. Station announcements are not human dialogue from Daniel/Tomas.

## Relative Height / Build

When Daniel and Tomas appear across comparable floor planes/counter scale, preserve stable relative adult size. Do not resize one character dramatically for romance framing.

## Blocking Rule

Any identity/topology failure is blocking even if story text, background, and overall composition are otherwise correct.
