# Ninth Hardening — Character Topology / Identity-Surface Addendum

This addendum extends the active `manhwa-2d-production-auditor` rules. It does not change story canon; it closes character-rendering loopholes involving broken anatomy, mirrored identity landmarks, dropped wardrobe/accessories, altered hair construction, age/build recasting, or local-color/material drift.

## Whole-Body Anatomical Topology — Absolute

Every visible character is one physically coherent body.

Reject:
- extra/missing/duplicated arms, hands, legs, feet, ears, fingers
- detached/floating body parts or hair masses
- impossible elbow/knee/wrist/ankle/shoulder/hip directions
- limb emerging from wrong torso side
- lower body incompatible with pelvis
- close-up limb incompatible with adjacent wide pose

## Limb Ownership Across Close-Ups / Reverse Shots — Absolute

A continuity-critical limb belongs to SAME character and SAME anatomical side. Preserve glove/sleeve/skin/accessory cues. Reverse shot may flip screen side; ownership may not.

## Daniel Permanent Identity — Absolute

Preserve:
- age 36
- warm brown skin
- long narrow adult face
- close-cropped black curls
- early temple silver
- one stable nose-bridge scar
- medium-tall slim specialist build
- rectangular work glasses when currently worn
- slate-blue chore-jacket identity

Reject teenage/idol recast, round-glasses identity, undercut/long hair, broad action-hero build, scar omission/migration, or glossy close-up recast.

## Tomas Permanent Identity — Absolute

Preserve:
- age 28
- sun-warm tan skin
- adult lean baker build
- long dark wavy hair past shoulders
- low nape/back tie with jade-green cord
- loose face-framing strands
- cream cafe shirt + brown waist apron work identity

Reject short/high-tied/bun/xianxia/aristocratic hair, schoolboy/idol recast, changing tie color, disappearing apron, or glossy fantasy rendering.

## Supporting Character Identity

When their canonical WebPs are used, preserve all established age/build/hair/accessory side locks for Maribel, Lilia, Benjamin, Arturo, and Ernesto. Do not “beautify” older characters into younger glamorous versions or turn grounded human antagonists into supernatural archetypes.

## Wardrobe Construction / Local-Color Continuity — Absolute

Within a continuous scene preserve the same physical clothes/colors/material family. Lighting may change value but not identity hue/construction.

## Clothing / Body Occlusion Topology — Absolute

Clothing/hair/apron/jacket/gloves/accessories wrap one continuous body. Occlusion cannot hide an extra/missing limb or impossible connection.

## Speaking / Listening / Mouth-State Logic — Absolute

Visible acting agrees with dialogue/audio source.

- listener does not mouth speaker's line
- offscreen Lilia remains offscreen unless explicitly shown
- station announcement is not Daniel/Tomas human speech
- physical writing/scratch text is not mouthed

## Character Material Continuity — Absolute

Skin, hair, cloth, glasses, jewelry, apron, shoes, and accessories retain flat matte material family across adjacent views. Reject glossy lips/hair/skin, neon accessories, mirror chrome, or local cinematic beautification.

## Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- extra/missing/duplicated body part appears
- limb ownership/anatomical side changes
- face/age/build/hair identity drifts
- Daniel scar/temple-silver/glasses identity changes
- Tomas hair length/low tie/jade cord/apron identity changes
- wardrobe/local colors change without legal cause
- clothing/hair occlusion hides impossible anatomy
- listener mouths wrong source
- local materials become glossy/cinematic

Rendered compliance remains pending until actual pixels are inspected.
