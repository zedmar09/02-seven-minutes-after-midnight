# Tenth Hardening — Pose / Performance Continuity Addendum

This addendum is always binding on active Manhwa production.

## Pose State Persistence — Absolute

Across adjacent views and strip boundaries, character support state persists until a visible action or legal cut changes it.

Track:
- standing / leaning / crouching / walking state
- weight-bearing leg / center of mass
- shoulder / hip / torso facing
- head orientation relative to torso
- arm / hand gesture
- counter/window distance
- hand height and support

A reverse camera cannot silently turn a body, move feet, change a lean, or reset posture.

## Relative Position / Scale

Daniel and Tomas retain stable world-space distance around the canonical counter/service window. Camera perspective may change screen size, not physical height/build or the width of the threshold between them.

## Emotional Continuity

Emotion progresses from real script causes and may not reset between camera cuts.

Chapter 1 performance spine:
1. Daniel procedural suspicion / dry detachment
2. wary exploration
3. activation shock
4. controlled disbelief + Tomas's humor
5. curiosity / chemistry
6. pain and caution at unstable contact
7. empirical wonder at object aging
8. tenderness around `I AM REAL.`
9. brief comedy with Lilia
10. urgency at 12:05 / Arturo clue
11. visible fear at 12:06
12. restrained tenderness in warm finger brush
13. abrupt loss at 12:07
14. broken laugh at Tomas reply
15. final stunned dread at fire warning

Reject unexplained panic→neutral→smile resets, glamour seduction, villain smirks, chibi shock, crying collapse, or emotion larger than the scripted stimulus.

## Gesture Continuity

Temporary gestures persist until visibly released/changed:
- Daniel's counter grip
- Tomas's tray support
- wrist catch and release
- note-writing hand
- page handoff
- apron-pocket storage
- raised hand after Tomas disappears

Do not invent gestures that conflict with occupied hands/props.

## Clothing-Layer / Wear State

Within the same midnight sequence preserve:
- Daniel jacket present/closure state as established
- work glasses on/off state only if visibly changed
- cotton glove wear state only if visibly changed
- Tomas shirt/apron and hair-tie state
- flour marks as temporary local detail only when compatible with canon

Camera cuts do not roll sleeves, remove glasses, untie hair, or clean/change apron automatically.

## Hair / Garment Motion

Hair and fabric respond to leaning, turning, steam, air movement, and gravity without becoming a redesign. A moving loose strand cannot change Tomas's entire hair length/tie construction.

## Seam Inheritance

Each next technical strip inherits previous approved strip's:
- pose/support
- shoulder/hip facing
- head orientation where relevant
- gesture/held posture
- relative distance/scale
- emotional intensity
- wardrobe layer state
- object hand occupancy

Only a legal cut or explicit first action may change them.

## Blocking Rule

Reject a render if camera change silently resets pose, facing, gesture, relative scale, emotional intensity, or clothing/hair state. Sequential production may not continue until repaired.
