# Tenth Hardening — Pose / Performance / Relative-Scale Continuity Addendum

This is the final preproduction hardening layer under `manhwa-2d-production-auditor`.

It closes the loophole where correct identity, objects, architecture, camera axis, and action mechanics can still be shown while character pose, scale, emotional performance, body facing, gesture, clothing-layer state, or hair/garment motion resets between adjacent views.

## Pose-State Conservation — Absolute

A camera cut is not a pose reset.

Track:
- standing / walking / crouching / leaning / bracing
- weight-bearing leg(s)
- shoulder/hip/torso orientation
- head turn relative to torso
- elbow/arm gesture
- hand height/purpose
- spine lean
- feet position
- center of mass/support against counter/floor

If only head/eyes turn, shoulders/hips/feet remain. If torso/full body turns, stage the motion or use a legal cut.

## Body-Facing / Shoulder-Hip Axis — Absolute

For stationary Daniel/Tomas dialogue at the counter/window:
- preserve story-world shoulder/sternum/hip/foot direction
- reverse shot may flip page direction but not silently rotate body
- head rotation may differ within believable neck range
- leaning over counter/window remains physically supported until released

## Relative Height / Build — Absolute

Canonical body relationships remain stable.

Daniel = medium-tall slim adult specialist.
Tomas = lean adult baker; shared-scene relative scale established canon-compatibly and then preserved.

Do not resize one character for romance/intimidation/composition. Perspective changes apparent screen size only.

## Expression / Emotion Continuity — Absolute

Emotion changes only because story provides a reason.

Chapter 1 baseline progression:
- archive/station entry: dry professional focus + skepticism
- Cafe Siete clues: analytical unease
- activation/Tomas reveal: startled controlled disbelief
- early banter: fear softened by dry/warm humor
- year/name proof: wonder + caution
- 12:02 instability/wrist catch: acute localized shock
- object-aging proof: serious impossible-evidence focus
- `I am real.` exchange: vulnerable warmth
- Lilia interruption: brief embarrassed/human relief
- 12:05: investigative urgency + private attraction under pressure
- Arturo clue: Daniel sharpens into evidence mode
- 12:06: Tomas's fear becomes undisguised; Daniel urgent
- tomorrow promise/second contact: shaken private tenderness
- 12:07 loss: abrupt absence, Daniel stunned
- returned note: broken laughter/controlled emotion
- final fire scratch: controlled dread, not melodramatic scream

No random smile→panic→neutral→seductive reset between camera views.

## Gesture Continuity — Absolute

Track and persist until visibly changed:
- Daniel flashlight handling
- glove state
- notebook/pencil handling
- pointing toward glass/clock
- Tomas tray/tongs handling
- leaning over counter/window
- wrist catch/release
- paper passing/receiving
- apron-pocket placement

Do not invent gestures that conflict with occupied hands.

## Clothing-Layer / Sleeve / Glove / Glasses State — Absolute

Within continuous Chapter 1 meeting:
- Daniel chore jacket remains same layer/construction
- glasses state remains where established unless visibly adjusted/removed
- cotton gloves remain exact current worn/off state
- Tomas shirt/apron remain same items
- Tomas sleeve roll state remains stable
- jade hair tie remains in place

Reverse shots cannot silently change any of these.

## Hair / Garment Motion — Absolute

Tomas's long hair may sway/loose strands move naturally but remains same length, wave family, low tie and jade cord. Daniel's close curls remain close-cropped. Jacket/apron folds may respond to pose without becoming different garments.

Motion cannot hide an unperformed body turn.

## Emotional / Pose Seam Rule — Absolute

At every strip boundary carry forward:
- body support/pose
- shoulder/hip facing
- head orientation when relevant
- gesture/held-item posture
- relative distance/scale
- emotional intensity
- clothing/sleeve/glove/glasses/hair state

Next strip changes these only through its first visible action or explicit legal cut.

## Automatic Reject Additions

Reject if:
- stationary character silently rotates torso/hips/feet
- head-only turn becomes unscripted full-body turn
- gesture changes without release/change
- relative body scale changes on same floor/counter plane
- emotional intensity resets/jumps without story cause
- ordinary adult performance becomes chibi/horror/glamour/villain acting without script
- clothing/sleeve/glove/glasses/apron state changes without action
- hair motion becomes redesign
- seam resets pose/facing/emotion rather than inheriting it

Rendered compliance remains pending until actual pixels are inspected.
