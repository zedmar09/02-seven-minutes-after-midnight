# San Aurelio Fire Conspiracy Bible — Creator Only

This is the creator solution to the human mystery. Do not reveal it wholesale in early reader-facing chapters.

## Fixed Event

**San Aurelio Junction Fire**
- date: November 23, 1986
- creator ignition time: `12:21:40 AM`
- first logged alarm / official start time: `12:23 AM`
- origin: service corridor behind/near obsolete electrical cable trunk and relay access
- official conclusion: accidental electrical fault in aging station wiring
- creator truth: deliberate arson staged to resemble an electrical failure

## Primary Motive

Arturo Salcedo represents redevelopment/land interests that cannot cheaply acquire the station property while the active railway easement, heritage protections, tenant leases, and surviving title/maintenance records remain intact.

A destructive 'electrical accident' serves three goals:
1. force emergency closure/condemnation
2. destroy records that obstruct the redevelopment acquisition
3. eliminate Tomas Rivera as a witness who has seen evidence tying Arturo's people to station-office and signal-system tampering

The fire was intended as a controlled destructive event, not a supernatural ritual.

## Culprit Chain

### Arturo Salcedo — director/fixer
- commissions the sabotage and cover story
- arranges forged maintenance access and political pressure
- wants the cafe/adjacent records destroyed
- knows Tomas has seen too much and treats his death as acceptable/desired
- does not personally need to light the fire

### Cesar Villanueva — executing electrical subcontractor
- age 39 in 1986
- railway electrical subcontractor with legitimate familiarity with relay/service spaces
- paid by Arturo through a shell maintenance contractor
- removes/changes relay components, creates the southbound signal fault, cuts the relevant feed, and rigs the ignition to leave electrical-looking damage
- creator-only direct physical arson executor

### Benjamin Manalo — coerced compromised witness
- receives a forged/pressured maintenance instruction
- unlocks the service corridor when he should not
- later falsifies part of his official patrol statement under threat
- keeps a private patrol notebook whose timing does not match the official record
- guilty and frightened, but not a willing arsonist

### Ernesto Galang — technical witness
- notices removed fuses/jumpers and an impossible maintenance configuration
- is diverted toward the southbound signal problem before the fire
- later keeps a hand-marked maintenance diagram because the official electrical explanation does not fit what he saw
- his 2026 memory is imperfect and partially manipulated by decades of official repetition

### Lilia Ramos — evidence protector
- notices Tomas's fear and Arturo's recurring presence
- hides evidence Tomas brings back rather than trusting station management
- survives the initial fire event but her later statement is selectively cut/rewritten in the surviving archive
- her hidden evidence path is one reason Cafe Siete remains central to the 2026 mystery

### Tomas Rivera — targeted witness
Tomas becomes a specific target because he:
- repeatedly serves Arturo during station-office visits
- notices meetings that are absent from official visitor records
- sees Cesar using access/routes that do not fit normal cafe/rail operations
- acquires/copies part of redevelopment Annex C
- recognizes that the failed southbound signal and service-corridor activity are connected
- refuses to surrender or forget what he has seen

## Fire-Night Chronology — November 23, 1986

Times below are creator authority and should be revealed gradually.

### Before Midnight

**11:52 PM** — Cesar enters using legitimate-looking subcontractor access. His presence is later omitted from the official visitor record.

**11:58 PM** — Arturo is still in/near station offices despite later claiming he left earlier.

### Midnight / Temporal Window

**12:00 AM** — Cafe Siete time-slip window opens according to whatever duration exists by this point in the series. Daniel/Tomas interaction on this future night must respect all accumulated ledger state.

### Sabotage Sequence

**12:07 AM** — Cesar cuts/disconnects the branch feeding the relevant ticket-hall clock/relay circuit. `CLOCK-STATION-01` stops at `12:07`. This is the mundane historical event that leaves the surviving 2026 station clock fixed at that time and becomes part of the supernatural wound's anchor.

**12:11 AM** — The southbound signal failure becomes operationally obvious. Staff attention is pulled away from the cafe/service side.

**12:14 AM** — Benjamin unlocks the service corridor under a forged/pressured maintenance instruction. His later official patrol statement moves this time and omits the requester.

**12:18 AM** — Tomas sees enough corridor activity to understand that the danger is deliberate. He scratches `FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.` through the painted Cafe Siete glass/sign layer using a steel key edge. This scratch is meant for future Daniel and becomes loop-locked evidence.

**12:20 AM** — Cesar completes an ignition assembly behind the obsolete cable-trunk/relay area using accelerant/heat plus deliberately damaged electrical components so post-fire inspection will support an electrical-fault story.

**12:21:40 AM** — Actual ignition.

**12:22:10 AM** — Smoke reaches the flour-room/kitchen side through real service ventilation/opening paths.

**12:23 AM** — Alarm is raised/logged. This becomes the official 'fire start' time in surviving public records.

**12:24–12:27 AM** — Service access and crowd movement become lethal. Tomas delays/changes his movement because of evidence and people in danger. Personal effects later become the basis of his official death identification.

## Tomas's Official Death Record — Important

The baseline archive treats Tomas as dead in the fire.

However, identification is **not medically conclusive**. It relies heavily on personal effects, witness assumptions, and a compromised casualty chain rather than definitive remains.

This keeps the story's eventual rescue problem difficult but not logically impossible.

Do not reveal this loophole too early.

## Why `12:07` Matters

`12:07` is not the fire ignition time.

It is the sabotage/power-cut time that:
- stops the station clock
- precedes the signal/corridor sequence
- becomes physically preserved in 2026
- aligns with Cafe Siete's anomalous seven-minute interval

The Cafe clock's older broken-at-12:07 state indicates the temporal wound has retrocausal behavior and is not merely a ghost replay of the fire.
