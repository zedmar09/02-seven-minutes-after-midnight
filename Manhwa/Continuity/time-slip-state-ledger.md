## Cost Rules

- Do not apply a listed cost early within the same chapter.
- Do not round a duration for convenience.
- The Cafe clock's second hand becomes mandatory when a close time is not on a whole minute.
- A later explicit story revision may change a cost only if this ledger, the chapter matrix, and all downstream chapter states are updated together.
