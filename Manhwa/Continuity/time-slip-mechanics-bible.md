# Time-Slip Mechanics Bible — Creator Only

This file is the permanent creator authority for the Cafe Siete connection unless explicitly revised.

## 1. Calendar Relationship

During the standard connection, Daniel and Tomas occupy the **same calendar month/day and clock time, exactly forty years apart**.

Chapter 1:
- Daniel: October 17, 2026
- Tomas: October 17, 1986

A successful next-night meeting normally advances both sides by one calendar day.

## 2. Standard Opening

The standard Cafe Siete connection begins at exactly `12:00:00 AM`.

Its original healthy duration is exactly seven minutes, closing at `12:07:00 AM`.

Historical interference and boundary damage can permanently shorten later windows. The current authoritative duration by chapter is stored in `time-slip-state-ledger.md`.

Lost time never silently recovers.

## 3. Two Separate Clocks — Absolute

There are two physically separate recurring clocks.

### `CLOCK-CAFE-01` — Cafe Siete clock
- brass-rimmed wall clock inside Cafe Siete
- has hour/minute hands and a readable second hand because seconds become story-critical from Chapter 4 onward
- in Tomas's ordinary 1986 experience it has been broken for roughly two years and normally rests at `12:07`
- on connection activation it resets to `12:00:00` and becomes the **connection-state clock**
- it advances continuously until the current allowed duration expires
- when the connection closes it returns/settles to the inactive `12:07` state

### `CLOCK-STATION-01` — San Aurelio ticket-hall clock
- separate larger station clock in the ticket hall
- in Daniel's 2026 baseline it is physically stopped at `12:07`
- creator cause: the fire-night sabotage cuts the relevant station feed at 12:07 on November 23, 1986
- it does **not** reset to 12:00 during Daniel/Tomas meetings

During an active interval, avoid showing both clocks in one composition unless the story explicitly needs the contrast. The Cafe clock is the countdown authority. The station clock is the historical clue.

## 4. Dual-Era Co-Registration

The connection does not create a fantasy portal or duplicate building.

Cafe Siete is one structural footprint with two historical states temporarily co-registered.

Daniel remains physically anchored to the 2026 state. Tomas remains physically anchored to the 1986 state.

They cannot walk freely into the other era.

## 5. Shared Counter — Unstable Overlap Surface

The main counter can briefly co-register across both eras.

It allows:
- both men to support opposite edges of the same inert tray for a moment
- transient material traces such as a fresh coffee ring to imprint and age on Daniel's side
- brief accidental fingertip overlap

It does **not** provide a reliable safe transfer route.

Prolonged living contact through the unstable counter overlap causes phase-shear pain. This is why Tomas catching Daniel's wrist hurts.

A close-up or reverse shot never changes this rule.

## 6. Service Window — Stable Aperture

The narrow service window is the stable exchange aperture.

While open it can safely pass:
- letters and paper
- a plate/saucer
- pastries/small food
- small hand-held objects
- fingertips, hands, and limited forearm contact

It cannot pass a full living body.

The field resists increasing living mass before the physical opening itself becomes the only limitation. A shoulder/chest attempt produces severe pressure/phase shear and damages the connection.

The service window therefore supports restrained hand intimacy but never becomes a free doorway.

## 7. Living Contact

### Unstable counter contact
- momentary fingertip contact may be tolerable
- sustained wrist/hand contact produces sharp pain
- pain is phase shear, not electricity and not a magical burn

### Service-window contact
- fingertips/hands can touch without the sharp counter pain
- warmth, pulse, fabric, pressure, and ordinary human sensation can transmit
- Chapter 1 final contact is Tomas's bare fingertips against Daniel's thin cotton-gloved fingertips; Daniel still perceives warmth through the glove

## 8. Object Crossing — Identity Never Duplicates

A crossing object remains one physical object.

For each crossing track:
1. object ID
2. origin era
3. source hand/surface
4. threshold entry
5. threshold exit
6. transformed age/state
7. destination hand/surface
8. later storage/history

### 1986 → 2026
The object arrives with approximately forty years of material aging appropriate to its actual composition and historical exposure.

### 2026 → 1986
The object initially appears unnaturally new. Once the interval closes, it becomes part of 1986 causal history and begins living forward normally from that date.

If that same object later reaches 2026, it carries the material history accumulated since 1986.

## 9. Closing-Boundary Transit

An object already inserted into the service-window threshold immediately before closure can complete transit **after the visual connection has sealed**.

This explains Chapter 1's returned warning page:
- Daniel sends it to Tomas at 12:06
- Tomas adds `I WILL BRING BETTER BREAD.`
- in the unseen final fraction before closure he pushes the same page back into the aperture
- the boundary closes while the page is in transit
- after settlement, the same page emerges on Daniel's side aged forty years

This is not spontaneous duplication and not a magical paper generator.

## 10. Historical Change vs Boundary Damage

Two different actions can reduce future connection time:

1. **Historical interference** — changing a third-party record, schedule, witness action, evidence survival path, or fixed historical outcome.
2. **Boundary damage** — forcing living mass or unstable contact beyond what the aperture safely supports.

Both are recorded in `time-slip-state-ledger.md`.

## 11. Fixed Point

The San Aurelio station fire is a strong fixed historical event.

The fixed point does not mean every casualty or every detail is immutable. It means direct attempts to erase the fire itself cause disproportionately large temporal damage and may erase the abandoned conditions that produce Cafe Siete's connection.

Daniel and Tomas can eventually search for a survival path that preserves enough of the fixed event to keep causal history coherent.

## 12. No Automatic Rule Exceptions

Do not introduce:
- free full-body crossing
- daytime openings
- arbitrary extra minutes
- recovered connection time
- remote connection from another building
- duplicate versions of a crossing object
- generic magical portal VFX

unless a later creator-only revision explicitly establishes the new mechanism and updates all affected ledgers.
