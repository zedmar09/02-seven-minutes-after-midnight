## Chapter 1 Archive Carrier Locks

`E-00` uses two distinct physical carriers:
- `REPORT-FIRE-01` — official report
- `DIAGRAM-MAINT-01` — legitimate protected maintenance diagram

They are not two views of the same document. Strip 002 must visibly bring them together before comparison.

`E-01` uses three distinct death-notice sheets in the same document family. Their same-width cut is the clue; they must not be rendered as one repeated collage sheet.

The recurring visual family is defined by `Manhwa/Object-References/san-aurelio-fire-evidence-document-system-canonical-reference-prompt.md` and its eventual approved WebP.

## Scratch Geometry / Visibility Lock

Both Chapter 1 scratches exist on the **same established painted Cafe Siete glass/sign zone** in Daniel's baseline 2026.

`TOMAS` is visible first.

`FIRE STARTS IN THE SERVICE CORRIDOR.` is lower/less obvious and obscured by grime, dust, damage, and viewing angle. Chapter 1 does **not** cause the warning to write itself into existence. The activation/shift merely makes Daniel notice what was physically present.

No glowing letters. No on-screen self-writing effect.

## Intentional Bootstrap Loop

`TOMAS` is an intentional stable causal loop:
1. Tomas scratches his name in late 1986 because Daniel told him he already saw it in 2026.
2. Daniel sees it in 2026 and tells Tomas on Oct 17.

The story may question the origin of the information, but production must not accidentally assign a different author.

The fire-warning scratch is also loop-sensitive but has an external factual trigger: Tomas observes the corridor sabotage on fire night and creates the warning because he knows future Daniel can read the glass.

## Evidence Reveal Discipline

Creator knowledge is not reader knowledge.

Strip 002 allows Daniel to conclude only that the official report and maintenance record conflict. He does not yet know the report was falsified by Arturo-linked influence, that Cesar is the physical executor, or the fire-night sabotage sequence.

Do not let Daniel conclude `Cesar Villanueva = arson executor` in Arc 1.
Do not reveal the full 12:07 → signal failure → corridor unlock → ignition chain in one early exposition scene.
Do not reveal the weak casualty identification as a guaranteed survival answer before the romance has paid the cost of believing Tomas will die.

## Audit Rule

Every new major clue must be added here with:
- physical carrier
- creator cause
- baseline/rewrite status
- chapter first appearance
- chapter allowed interpretation
- final solution relationship

No atmospheric clue without causal purpose.
