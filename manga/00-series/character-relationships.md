# Character Relationships

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

The archive prelude changes Daniel's knowledge: an inventory becomes a suspected pattern of deliberate omission. Its exact prose is preserved. The station scene changes world knowledge, fear, trust, and goals: Daniel meets Tomas, both test material contact, exchange names and evidence, choose a second meeting, and leave Daniel with a tangible reply and a personal warning.

Daniel keeps the dry professional humor, gloves and conservation habits. His broken laugh is not replaced with invented grief exposition. Tomas keeps initiative, teasing, bread-making competence, Lilia's chosen-family relationship and the decision to reply. His future aim of protecting Lilia and the cafe's people is an accepted development direction, not an added secret or a finished future arc.

Lilia and Arturo are the names to use in eventual derivatives. Inez/Rafael in old page prompts remain historical source errors, not aliases. Those original prompts are untouched. No bibles, manga scripts or reference artwork are adopted in this step.

## Protected Relationship Material

Protect the opening's paper-based worldview, dry institutional humor, close Daniel viewpoint, restrained adult attraction, practical cafe language, and tactile details. Preserve the intent and recognizable phrasing of these existing anchors: Daniel trusting paper more than people; the museum banter; I am real / Then I am real too; bread turning elderly; I will bring better bread.

Protect the sequence from archival suspicion to impossible contact, reciprocal proof, a return promise, closure, the aged reply, and finally the warning. Keep the first archival sleeve distinct from the later notebook page. Preserve Lilia's chosen-family exchange and the ordinary work continuing around the miracle.

Do not add a dead lover, a traumatic family history, a new supernatural ability, an alias, a second broker, or secret notebook contents as an explanation unless separately proposed and approved. Repeated hair or warmth details may be trimmed only when they repeat the same function, not simply because repetition exists.
