# Tone And Genre

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

Primary genre: adult BL time-slip romance. Secondary genres: historical mystery and emotional thriller. The audience hypothesis is older-teen and adult readers interested in restrained queer intimacy, tangible clues, and a bounded supernatural premise. No reader gender, commercial market size, or genre-sales assumption is established.

Provisional editorial guidance is 16+ for suspense, death/fire themes, and brief pain, with restrained intimacy and non-graphic violence in the current scope. It is not a platform rating. The characters' adult ages, Daniel 36 and Tomas 28 in their respective eras, come from the reference text; queer romance does not itself require an explicit-content rating.

Proposed positioning rests on the actual combination of paper conservation, baked offerings, a narrow exchange window, and civic erasure. Do not claim the time-slip device is unprecedented or claim originality clearance. Differentiation notes are author intent, not evidence of external comparison.

Preserve chapter-based prose and prefer English left-to-right paged monochrome manga, with color covers treated separately. Reading direction is a reversible recommendation; neither fourteen interior pages nor the runtime pixel dimensions are locked production specifications. Production quality profile is high. Visual production remains external to Codex and requires the later approval sequence.

Keep the existing fifteen-title first arc as a provisional planning envelope. Use the opening three-chapter sequence as a bounded development checkpoint after sources and continuation decisions are ready. Treat 105 chapters as a horizon to justify, not a quota. Further cafes or memory-reset arcs must change the central relationship and fire investigation.

Recommend an earned hopeful relationship resolution with meaningful cost. This is a proposed tonal goal, not a chosen finale mechanism, a guarantee of survival, or a public promise. Do not advertise a happy or tragic ending until it is actually selected. Protect the existing cultural cues while leaving the country unnamed in the Chapter 1 repair; historical specificity requires supplied evidence or separately authorized research.
