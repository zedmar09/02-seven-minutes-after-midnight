# Chapter 1 Parallel Events

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

Source basis: Chapter 1 lines 51, 107, 133-139, 243-265; SMA-ISS-005/006, accepted SMA-PROP-003/004.

The customer counter and the back-wall kitchen hatch are different surfaces. A kitchen doorway beside the hatch makes Tomas's route explicit. At first Daniel stands on the customer side of the counter in ruined 2026; Tomas stands on its working side in 1986. The shared contact surfaces permit sight, sound and limited contact, not bodily passage into another year.

After the counter flickers at 12:02, Tomas takes his tray through his own kitchen doorway. Daniel collects his folder and flashlight and walks around the end of his own ruined counter. He remains on the cafe side of the back-wall hatch. Tomas faces him from the kitchen side. Daniel's feet never leave his ruined present. This is accepted new choreography, not a recovered source event.

Light, heat and sounds can reach Daniel, but his own stools, tile and painted door never become restored. The 1986 kitchen and case are visible beyond the shared surfaces. At closure the past view vanishes; his furniture does not break a second time. Exact dimensions, screen direction and panel geometry remain Phase 5 work.
