## Purpose

Create one reusable prop board for the physical objects handled during the first seven-minute contact. The board must let later panels preserve design, scale, age state, damage, ownership, and the distinction between incomplete shared possession, backward passage, and completed forward transfer.

## Authority And Limits

1. [Cafe and archive props](cafe-and-archive-props.md) controls custody and state changes.
2. The active Chapter 1 manuscript controls appearance details and handling order.
3. The visual board cannot establish a universal time-transfer law beyond what Chapter 1 demonstrates.
4. Exact handwritten messages remain lettering content, not generated prop art.

No image attachment is currently required. A future correction may use an approved clock/sign/door board only if scale coordination is needed.

## Generation Brief

Produce ONE 1536 x 1024 landscape PNG named `time-transfer-props.png`. Draw a clean, high-detail black-and-white manga prop sheet with exactly eight isolated study groups on plain white paper. Every object must use a consistent real-world scale and three-quarter product-study angle unless a paired state requires a direct side-by-side match.

The eight study groups are:

1. A coin-sized brass Cafe Siete token, cup emblem side and number 7 side, with green corrosion translated into stippled dark oxidation along one edge. Leave any circular word band blank for later exact lettering.
2. Daniel's practical present-day flashlight, shown intact and switched off, durable enough for abandoned-building inspection.
3. Daniel's permit folder with unused acid-free paper sleeves, plus one ordinary graphite pencil. Keep the sleeve and notebook paper visually distinct.
4. Tomas's 1986 serving tray with brass tongs, practical cafe construction, sized for several small rolls.
5. One fresh sugared roll on a clean white saucer, paired with the exact same roll and saucer after forty years of forward aging: shrunken dark cracked bread, dulled sugar, yellowed saucer rim, and one hairline glaze fracture.
6. One blank acid-free archival sleeve in crisp present-day condition, paired with the same paper after backward passage still white, crisp, and unchanged. No handwriting.
7. One torn notebook page in clean present-day condition, paired with the same page after returning forward: yellowed, brittle, curled, and structurally identical. No handwriting.
8. Tomas's small 1986 apron pencil paired with Daniel's snapped pencil tip as two clearly different tools. Do not merge them into one shared pencil.

## State Rules

- The tray remains in 1986 and never receives forty years of aging.
- The roll and saucer remain fresh while both men share possession across the hatch. Aging begins only after Tomas releases and Daniel draws them fully into 2026.
- The acid-free sleeve sent backward stays crisp.
- The returned notebook page ages only after it clears the opening into 2026.
- The first sleeve note and the later notebook page are different objects with different paper construction.
- Do not depict a second transfer, recovery, biological aging, injury, or a permanent time deduction.

## Printed Manga Reference Finish

Create a finished clean black-and-white human-drawn 2D manga production sketch/reference sheet on white paper. Use precise variable ink contours, small flat solid-black shapes, restrained discrete screentone, and sparse functional hatching. Express paper fiber, wood grain, metal wear, ceramic crazing, bread crumb, sugar, and corrosion through contour and flat print texture. Every paired state must preserve identical geometry so the transformation reads as age, not substitution.

More resolved than a rough thumbnail or construction sketch, never glossy product art. No rough construction residue, color, tint, sepia, gloss, reflections, photographic shadow, glow, bloom, rim light, cinematic contrast, lens effects, blur, smooth gradient, airbrush, painterly grayscale, photorealism, CGI, 3D rendering, smoke aura, labels, dimension arrows, captions, SFX, logo, signature, or watermark.

- No readable words except the numeral 7 on the token. The exact `CAFE SIETE` ring text must be added later as controlled lettering.
- No decorative antique styling that makes Daniel's present-day tools look from 1986.
