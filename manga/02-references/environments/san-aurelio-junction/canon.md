# San Aurelio Junction: Canon Status

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

Status: not adopted. Historical architectural details remain source intent; they have not been independently researched.

Source basis: Chapter 1 lines 19-29, 41-45, 133-173, 191-223, 373-389, 453-473; SMA-ISS-011/012/013/014/030, accepted SMA-PROP-009/011.

Daniel already knows the fire report names the service corridor and that wiring records contradict it. He does not know who removed the name strips, who scratched the glass, whether Tomas died, or who caused the fire. His initial guess of 1986 comes from the fire files; Tomas's answer supplies the encounter's stated date. The late-access permit is an accepted small addition that creates a practical deadline, not a reason he knew the cafe would open.

Mutual introductions precede the scratched-name conversation. Tomas denies writing it; Daniel's suggestion that he may do so later remains speculation. Daniel records the failed southbound signal and Arturo's name. Redevelopment makes him attentive to a broker, not certain of guilt. Tomas's playful "That is what you heard?" still answers Daniel ignoring the compliment.

The closing scratch makes an existing archival problem frighteningly personal. It does not newly teach Daniel the corridor location, prove murder, prove Tomas's death, or identify the author. The first photograph must be compared during a later approved continuation; no photograph contents or changed-message proof are invented in this repair.
