# Page Composition Rules

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. The author-approved visual-quality direction below does not approve a storyboard, artwork or production release.

Future nemu is geometry-only, with explicit left-to-right reading sequence and event-driven page layouts. No exact page geometry or panel count is approved. The fourteen legacy interior prompts do not constitute the current storyboard.

Use the supplied manga examples as composition principles, not assets to copy: establish a clear dominant panel or beat, vary panel area with narrative weight, control eye travel, and let quiet reactions and time-pressure moments breathe. Avoid uniform five-to-six-panel density, repetitive strip grids, decorative fragmentation, or detail that obscures the active story beat.

The Chapter 1 planning target is provisionally 36 interior pages within a 32-40 page working range. Page count follows pacing evidence; it is not a quota and remains subject to storyboard review. There is no fixed total panel count for the chapter or fixed panel count per page.

## Dynamic Panel Geometry

Break an initial panel allocation when the event needs a different rhythm. A quiet exchange may expand into fewer, larger panels; clock pressure, simultaneous reactions, physical action or a layered reveal may use more fragments. Every panel still carries one dominant event, and visual complexity must make the beat easier to read rather than merely busier.

Controlled overlap is permitted for intense pages. Approved forms include inset panels, diagonal or irregular clip polygons, foreground subjects crossing a frame edge, panel-on-panel overlays and bleed elements. Use them selectively for emphasis, simultaneity, impact, interruption or a deliberate shift in temporal perception.

Every overlap must define an explicit left-to-right reading sequence, base frame, optional clip polygon, z-index, border or bleed state, focus point and dialogue-safe zones. Do not cover critical faces, hands, props, clues, action paths, clock states or the next reading entry. Preserve gutters wherever separation is necessary for comprehension.

Panel art remains text-free and borderless. Overlap, clipping, borders and final stacking belong to the approved panel plan and deterministic page composition, not to baked-in generated artwork.

The retained deliverable is panel-first: approve text-free panel returns, then compose and letter separately. Matching another story's filenames does not switch this project to complete-page generation.
