# Page Standard

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. The visual-quality direction is user-approved; this standard does not approve a storyboard, artwork or production release.

English, left-to-right, paged monochrome; quality profile: high. Use 1654 x 2339 as the current high-resolution working target, not as a final print specification.

Apply the reference story's production discipline without copying its characters, layouts, assets or approvals. Apply the user's supplied manga examples as principles of visual hierarchy: variable panel area, decisive establishing or action panels, readable reaction beats, controlled eye flow, and composition matched to the event rather than a fixed grid.

The Chapter 1 planning target is provisionally 36 interior pages within a 32-40 page working range. The retired fourteen-page, 75-panel plan is too compressed for the approved quality direction and remains historical evidence only. No replacement total panel count is fixed: panel density may rise or fall with the event, provided the storyboard records the rationale and preserves clarity.

Controlled overlap is allowed for narratively intense compositions, including insets, diagonals, foreground frame breaks, overlays and bleed elements. Each overlapping element requires explicit frame or clip geometry, z-index, overlap permission, focus point, reading sequence and dialogue-safe zones. Composition must preserve all continuity-critical information and must never use overlap as decoration that makes the page harder to follow.

Retain the requested panel-first workflow. A panel is not a finished page. Approve panel artwork before composition and separate lettering. No page or panel jobs are released.

Panel artwork prohibits:

- dialogue
- captions
- balloons
- SFX text
- borders
- page numbers
- signatures
- watermarks
- color

Story, storyboard, references and production approvals are still required. Codex must never generate, edit, retouch or correct artwork.

The approved shared Daniel-and-Tomas image governs lead identity only. It must not override manuscript wardrobe, pose, props, environment, clock state, dates, signage, newspaper copy or title text. Future jobs must name its exact approved scope rather than asking for the whole image to be reproduced.

A page review must hold any return with wrong story beats, altered dialogue, speaker mismatch, character-identity drift, anatomy failure, missing or misplaced props, broken panel geometry, undeclared or obstructive overlap, period errors, clock or object-custody discontinuity, unclear left-to-right reading order, unplanned camera changes, missing panels, duplicated panels, signatures, watermarks or generation artifacts.
