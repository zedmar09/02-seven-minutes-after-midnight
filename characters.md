# Characters and Continuity — Seven Minutes After Midnight

This file is the story/character continuity authority for prose planning and active Manhwa production. Update it whenever a recurring character appears, a permanent visual identifier changes deliberately, an object gains time-slip importance, or the midnight-cafe rules are clarified.

`Character-References/*.webp`, once approved and committed, are the absolute visual identity authority for generation. This Markdown defines the intended canon those cards must encode.

## Production Visual Standard — Absolute

All recurring characters are rendered as **strict flat 2D human-drawn full-color Korean manhwa/webtoon characters** with clean linework, flat matte colors, restrained hard-edged cel shading, stable adult anatomy, natural hands, and consistent permanent landmarks.

Reject photorealism, semi-photorealism, 3D/CGI, glossy skin/hair, beauty-ad shine, painterly rendering, airbrushed gradients, cinematic grading, bloom, lens flare, depth-of-field blur, or generic AI-pretty recasting.

Expressions change on the same face. Reverse shots may flip screen-left/right, but permanent anatomical side, hair construction, scars, accessories, body proportions, and wardrobe construction do not mirror or redesign themselves.

## Visual Differentiation Standard

`Seven Minutes After Midnight` is an adult BL time-slip railway romance and historical mystery. Its cast and visual world must remain distinct from other story-library silhouettes.

Do not introduce palace court robes, royal marriage imagery, xianxia warrior hair, ghost-king masks, gothic mourning cloaks, teenage station uniforms, supernatural conductor motifs, death tickets, loyalty percentages, debt-box overlays, corporate-revenge framing, or generic modern-idol polish.

San Aurelio Junction is a grounded historical railway station shown in 1986 and 2026. The same architecture persists while age, fire damage, abandonment, restoration state, furniture, signage, crowds, and lighting differ by era.

# Core Cast

## Daniel Soriano

- Role: Main protagonist; present-day museum conservator; first person in decades to see the midnight cafe reopen.
- Age: 36 in 2026.
- Occupation: Paper and photograph conservator assigned to the San Aurelio Railway Archive restoration project.
- Personality: Patient, precise, lonely without admitting it, emotionally cautious, dryly funny under stress, and stubborn when physical evidence contradicts official history.
- Story Function: Investigates the station fire, falls in love with Tomas through the seven-minute connection, and must decide whether preserving history matters when history is the thing that killed the man he loves.

### Canonical Visual Identity

- adult man; never teenage or idol-young
- warm brown skin
- medium-tall, slim/elegant adult build
- long narrow face
- tired dark eyes
- close-cropped black curls
- early silver threaded at both temples; stable pattern family, not full gray hair
- one small healed horizontal/diagonal cut across the bridge of the nose; preserve exact canonical location once card is approved
- thin rectangular amber-tinted conservation glasses used while working; glasses must remain rectangular, never round as identity
- careful shoulders/hands/posture around fragile objects

### Primary Work Outfit

- slate-blue chore jacket
- neutral shirt
- dark trousers
- soft-soled practical shoes
- white cotton conservation gloves available/tucked when off duty and worn for evidence handling

### Signature Props

- cotton conservation gloves
- soft conservation brush
- archival tweezers
- magnifying loupe
- acid-free folders/sleeves
- notebook and pencil
- flashlight
- restored newspaper fragments
- brass Cafe Siete token after discovery

### Performance Lock

Daniel's hands reveal emotion. Normally he handles objects gently and deliberately. Fear for Tomas may make him grip the counter or notebook harder, but he does not become a melodramatic action lead. His baseline is analytical restraint escalating into controlled emotional vulnerability.

### Continuity Gates

Reject if Daniel:
- loses or relocates the nose-bridge scar
- changes from close-cropped curls to undercut/long hair
- loses the early-silver temple identity
- becomes broad/muscular action-hero build
- becomes teenage/doll-like
- switches rectangular glasses to round frames without story cause
- gains glossy/cinematic skin/hair treatment
- handles conservation evidence barehanded when the script explicitly establishes gloves

# Relationship Continuity

## Daniel ↔ Tomas

- Daniel initially approaches Tomas as impossible evidence made living.
- Tomas initially treats Daniel as a strange midnight customer, then a secret friend, then the person who hears him without requiring performance.
- Their romance grows through tiny acts: best pastry saved, carefully preserved notes, work stories, exchanged evidence, interrupted jokes, proximity at the service window, and hands reaching across a boundary too small for a body.
- They can make limited hand contact through the threshold. Full-body crossing is forbidden under current rules.
- Daniel's central fear: saving Tomas may remove the abandoned cafe and erase their connection/memories.
- Tomas's central fear: refusing to change history turns every tender midnight into a countdown to a death already recorded.
- Keep adult emotional restraint. Do not use chibi shock, sparkle romance, generic blush scenes, possessive melodrama, or instant erotic glamour to replace actual relationship progression.

# World and Environment Continuity

## San Aurelio Junction

A once-important railway station built in the 1930s.

Permanent architectural identity includes:
- arched windows
- green-and-cream tile family
- iron structural elements
- brass signs/details
- ticket hall
- platform clocks
- stained-glass transoms where established
- rain gutters
- Cafe Siete beside the old ticket hall

### 1986 State

Busy, staffed, lit, intact, station announcements active, passengers present, functional signage/furniture, coffee/steam, ordinary wear rather than ruin.

### 2026 State

Abandoned/partly cordoned, dusty, broken tile, redevelopment barriers/banners, plywood, rust, plaster, archive access/evidence, rain intrusion, damaged glazing, cold darkness.

The two eras retain the same underlying structural geometry unless a documented later modification explains a difference.

## Cafe Siete

Permanent locks:
- same location inside San Aurelio Junction
- same counter footprint/relationship
- same narrow service-window opening
- same clock/menu-wall relationship
- same structural door/window routes

1986 is alive, warm, functional, and full of food/service objects. 2026 is abandoned, damaged, stripped, dusty, and partly fire-scarred. Do not treat them as unrelated cafe designs.

## Service Window — Absolute

- narrow opening between kitchen and counter
- reliable small-object exchange point
- enough for plate, note, or hands
- not large enough for a human body
- physical opening/location remains same across eras
- 2026 bottom edge is charred where Chapter 1 establishes it
- 1986 counterpart is intact/polished in Chapter 1

## Counter

During the active connection the counter can become a shared physical surface across eras. Coffee rings/scratches/contact may cross time. Same-object/same-surface logic applies; do not create two incompatible counter geometries.

## Cafe Clock

- brass-rimmed wall clock associated with Cafe Siete
- Chapter 1 2026 pre-connection state: stopped at `12:07`
- at activation: hand snaps to `12:00`
- then advances 12:01 → 12:02 → ... → 12:07
- clock state is story evidence; never reset/skip/reverse without script
- connection degradation in later chapters may reduce available time; lost minutes are not cosmetic

# Time-Slip Object Continuity

Every exchanged object needs provenance:

`ORIGIN ERA → ORIGINAL OWNER/LOCATION → THRESHOLD ACTION → TRANSFORMATION → DESTINATION OWNER/LOCATION → PERSISTENT RESULT`

## Forward 1986 → 2026

Ordinary objects experience roughly forty years of aging after crossing unless the active interval temporarily suspends it. Preserve the same object identity while changing material state.

Examples:
- paper yellows; ink fades; edges soften/brittle
- pastries dry/shrink/crack
- photographs warp/fade
- metal tarnishes/oxidizes
- ceramic may craze/yellow rather than transform into a different object

## Backward 2026 → 1986

Objects initially look unnaturally new for the period. Once connection closes they may begin adapting toward historically plausible aging/state. Do not instantly redesign modern objects into 1986 equivalents unless the script explicitly establishes that behavior.

# Dialogue / Acting Ownership

Human speech, narration, internal thought, station announcements, and physical written text must remain distinct.

- offscreen Lilia audio does not create a visible speaking Lilia unless explicitly scripted
- station announcements remain environmental audio from the 1986 station system
- scratched glass warnings are physical text on Daniel's 2026 glass
- notebook/sleeve text is physical handwriting on the same paper object
- a close-up of text is a same-object representation, not a duplicate paper
- listener mouths must not appear to speak another character's line

# Canonical Asset Status

Character prompt specifications may exist before approved visual canonicals. Until the required `.webp` for a visible recurring character is generated, visually approved, converted, and committed, rendered-strip production that requires that character is blocked.
