## Executive Assessment

The opening is worth preserving. Its strongest material is the meeting itself: an evidence-minded conservator and a teasing baker establish trust through a ruined pastry, two different notes, and a narrow opportunity to touch. The voice combines occupational precision, dry humor, and restrained vulnerability. A focused repair is better supported than a wholesale rewrite.

The immediate problems are traceable. Page 10 substitutes Inez for Lilia; Page 11 substitutes Rafael Aragon for Arturo Salcedo; the page scripts omit the mutual introduction needed for the scratched-name clue. The counter/window geometry, extent of the time overlap, object protection, post-closure reply, and warning costs need consistent private rules. The final warning should make the recorded fire personal without prematurely proving Tomas's death or murder.

The proposed 105-chapter series has not been demonstrated by the available material. One complete prose chapter exists here. Later arcs need distinct consequences and payoffs so they do not repeat first discoveries, reset intimacy, or dilute San Aurelio into interchangeable cafe cases. These are outline risks, not claims about unwritten chapters' quality.

## Strengths to Preserve

1. Daniel's paper-based worldview and institutional humor. His methods make both mystery and intimacy specific to this protagonist (chapter.md:11-29).
2. Tomas's hospitality and reciprocal initiative. He offers bread, asks for a return object, and chooses to meet again; he is already more than a passive endangered lead (chapter.md:265-337, 401-427).
3. Tangible emotional evidence. The pastry, too-white archival sleeve, and aged notebook reply make time and trust visible. Keep the two pieces of paper distinct.
4. The chosen-family interruption. Lilia's kitchen voice makes the past a working social world, not merely a romantic backdrop (chapter.md:345-353).
5. The ending's sequence of tenderness followed by danger. The reply pays off recognition before the scratch raises a harder question; preserve that emotional order.
6. Grounded civic stakes. The archive's damaged records and redevelopment pressure connect personal loss to institutional erasure without requiring a large mythology.

Intended readers: older teens and adults drawn to restrained intimacy and evidence-driven suspense, an untested audience hypothesis. Provisional 16+ editorial guidance reflects death/fire themes and danger, not the genders of the couple; a formal rating depends on future content and venue.

Preserve chapter-based prose and a paged monochrome manga adaptation with English lettering and separate color covers. Left-to-right is a reversible working recommendation because no source direction is specified. The runtime page dimensions are placeholders, not an approved print specification. Production quality profile is high, but there is no ready production job.

Keep Arc 1's existing 15-chapter outline as a provisional planning envelope and develop a coherent opening three-chapter sequence after approvals. Treat 105 chapters as an aspiration until distinct arc payoffs and production capacity justify it. Do not commit to a release date or cadence yet.

Recommended success means understandable knowledge and time rules, meaningful change in every scene, mutual character agency, earned relationship/fire payoffs, preserved author voice, and a sustainable completion process.
