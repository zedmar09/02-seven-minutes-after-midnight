# Chapter 1 Visual-Quality Direction v002

Status: user-directed refinement of the accepted Chapter 1 visual-quality direction. This supersedes the composition guidance in v001 without changing the approved manuscript, provisional page range, production mode, canon state, stage locks, storyboard state or image permissions.

## User Direction

Panel totals may be broken according to the situation. Panels may overlap when necessary. The intended quality is intense, expressive manga page design in which geometry follows the event rather than a fixed count or uniform grid.

## Observed Design Principles

1. A page can shift abruptly between a dominant impact image, narrow reaction cuts, close-ups, wide context and small explanatory beats.
2. Panel density can change within the same sequence. The number of panels serves pacing, simultaneous action, emotional escalation and information load rather than a predetermined total.
3. Diagonal divisions, irregular crops, inset panels, foreground frame breaks and layered panels can intensify impact or connect simultaneous beats.
4. Black fields, white space and panel scale create rhythm. Complexity is strongest when one focal event still controls the page.
5. Overlap works only when the eye path remains deliberate and critical faces, gestures, props, clues and dialogue routes stay unobstructed.

## Current Approved Direction

- Keep the provisional 36-interior-page target and 32-40 page working range.
- Do not set a total Chapter 1 panel quota or a fixed panels-per-page rule.
- Allow the storyboard to increase or decrease panels by page or sequence when a documented pacing, clarity, suspense, intimacy, action or reveal need justifies it.
- Allow insets, diagonal or irregular clip polygons, foreground frame breaks, panel-on-panel overlays and bleed elements when they strengthen a specific beat.
- Require every panel to retain one dominant event even on dense pages.
- Require every overlap to declare its rectangular frame, optional clip polygon, z-index, border or bleed state, overlap permission, focus point, reading sequence and dialogue-safe zones.
- Keep critical faces, hands, props, action paths, clues, clock states and reader entry points clear.
- Preserve English left-to-right reading and the existing `panel_first` workflow. Generated panel art remains text-free and borderless; clipping, stacking, borders and lettering belong to later deterministic stages.
