# Page Composition Rules

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. The author-approved visual-quality direction below does not approve a storyboard, artwork or production release.

Future nemu is geometry-only, with explicit left-to-right reading sequence and event-driven page layouts. No exact page geometry or panel count is approved. The fourteen legacy interior prompts do not constitute the current storyboard.

Use the supplied manga examples as composition principles, not assets to copy: establish a clear dominant panel or beat, vary panel area with narrative weight, control eye travel, and let quiet reactions and time-pressure moments breathe. Avoid uniform five-to-six-panel density, repetitive strip grids, decorative fragmentation, or detail that obscures the active story beat.

The Chapter 1 planning target is provisionally 36 interior pages within a 32-40 page working range. Page count follows pacing evidence; it is not a quota and remains subject to storyboard review.

The retained deliverable is panel-first: approve text-free panel returns, then compose and letter separately. Matching another story's filenames does not switch this project to complete-page generation.
