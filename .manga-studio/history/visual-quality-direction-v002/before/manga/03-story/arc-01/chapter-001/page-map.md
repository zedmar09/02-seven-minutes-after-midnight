# Chapter 1 Page Map

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. The manuscript and visual-quality direction are approved; this document is not an approved storyboard and does not release production.

Status: ready for gated storyboard planning after canon and prerequisite stage approvals. The approved planning target is 36 interior pages, with a 32-40 page working range. The exact page count, panel allocation, reading geometry and page-turn plan remain unapproved until a versioned storyboard is reviewed.

The retired fourteen-page plan compressed 75 panels into an average of 5.36 panels per page. It is evidence only and must not be released or used as the current page map.

The future storyboard must record page purpose, dominant beats, left-to-right reading sequence, camera and staging, dialogue and structured SFX assignments, continuity requirements, and page-turn intent. Panel count must follow the scene's event density rather than a fixed grid.
