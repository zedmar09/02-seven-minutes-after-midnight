# World Rules

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

Source basis: Chapter 1 lines 51, 107, 133-139, 243-265; SMA-ISS-005/006, accepted SMA-PROP-003/004.

The customer counter and the back-wall kitchen hatch are different surfaces. A kitchen doorway beside the hatch makes Tomas's route explicit. At first Daniel stands on the customer side of the counter in ruined 2026; Tomas stands on its working side in 1986. The shared contact surfaces permit sight, sound and limited contact, not bodily passage into another year.

After the counter flickers at 12:02, Tomas takes his tray through his own kitchen doorway. Daniel collects his folder and flashlight and walks around the end of his own ruined counter. He remains on the cafe side of the back-wall hatch. Tomas faces him from the kitchen side. Daniel's feet never leave his ruined present. This is accepted new choreography, not a recovered source event.

Light, heat and sounds can reach Daniel, but his own stools, tile and painted door never become restored. The 1986 kitchen and case are visible beyond the shared surfaces. At closure the past view vanishes; his furniture does not break a second time. Exact dimensions, screen direction and panel geometry remain Phase 5 work.

The accepted working rule distinguishes incomplete/shared possession from completed transfer. The repaired scene demonstrates it; Daniel does not deliver a lecture or claim a tested universal law. Backward paper remains in its present condition. The first note and returned notebook page are distinct objects. A second passage of an already-aged object, biological transfer, unlimited preservation, and the role of the token have not been tested or decided.

## Undecided Rules

Keep ordinary daily recurrence as the accepted direction, not proof supplied by the first encounter. Before drafting Chapter 2, settle a private calendar: Daniel's date, each side's progression, the historical fire deadline, and the number of meetings available. Before a costly intervention, settle the exact deduction rule and amounts. First contact retains seven minutes; ordinary flicker is not automatically permanent loss. The first historical-warning penalty, recovery mechanics and coded-message edge cases are not invented here.

Preserve memory and agency consequences before using any reset. Do not repeat the pastry test or discovery of falsified records as new revelations. The first three units are a development checkpoint, not authorization to draft missing units. Fifteen titles are a provisional arc envelope; 105 chapters remain conditional. New cafes and ending mechanisms require their own decisions before being relied upon.
