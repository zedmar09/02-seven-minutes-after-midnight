# Format and Production Policy

English source and output, left-to-right paged manga, monochrome interiors and high-quality future production are the accepted directions. Color covers may be planned separately; no cover or panel job is released here.

`manga/01-style/production-policy-v001.json` records the user's existing requirements. It is not a visual-reference lock, a new style bible, an image job or proof of approved artwork. The configured 1654 x 2339 size remains a runtime placeholder, not an approved print specification.

Future panel art must exclude dialogue, captions, balloons, SFX text, borders, page numbers, signatures, watermarks and color. Lettering is separate. Nemu must be geometry-only, with event-driven pages, explicit reading order, motivated camera choices and fully structured SFX. References and jobs still require their appropriate approvals and locks.
