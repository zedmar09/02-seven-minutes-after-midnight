# Arc 1: The Fire At San Aurelio Junction

Working reference assembled from this story's existing material. This reorganization does not adopt canon, approve the manuscript, or release production.

Repair and strengthen the existing opening while preserving its authorial identity, then use it to guide a sustainable romantic mystery. The next prose deliverable is a new version of the one supplied Chapter 1, not a reconstruction of unavailable chapters. The fourteen legacy page scripts are comparison material for later adaptation. Chapter 2 is recorded as unavailable; the other chapters and arcs are plans.

Audit basis: .manga-studio/analysis/diagnostics/sma-comprehensive-audit-v001.json. The approval is separately recorded in .manga-studio/approvals/approval-8a2e8e6a05744edd9034972bf780a058.json. Evidence paths, line ranges, and stable IDs remain in the audit and the revision plan.

The source premise follows Daniel Soriano, a museum conservator in 2026, and Tomas Rivera, a baker in 1986, who meet for seven minutes through a railway cafe. The larger planned conflict is that saving Tomas from a recorded station fire may destroy the connection through which they fall in love. The supplied first chapter establishes the meeting and danger, not proof of Tomas's death or guilt in the fire.

Proposed reader promise: two adult men make something real through brief meetings, work, humor, and exchanged objects. Each attempt to understand the past or protect one another changes what they can know, risk, and choose together.

The proposed thematic focus is preservation versus agency: keeping evidence is not the same as keeping a person safe, and loving someone requires respecting choices that cannot be controlled. This is an interpretation recommended for development, not a claim that the author has already declared a message.
